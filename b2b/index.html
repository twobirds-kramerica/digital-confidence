What You Get

  • 🎓
    Self-paced, no instructor needed — members learn at their own pace, anytime, on any device. Staff time investment is minimal.
  • 👓
    Accessible design for seniors — adjustable text size, high-contrast mode, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and iPad-first layout. Built to accessibility standards from day one.
  • 🍁
    Canadian content, Ontario-specific — all examples, phone numbers, resources, and references are Canadian. Members won't encounter unfamiliar US services or language.
  • 💰
    Free to access — the base programme is publicly available at no cost. No accounts, no logins, no barriers for your members.
  • 🎨
    Customisable with your branding — Partner and Enterprise tiers include white-label options so members see your organisation's name and colours alongside the content.
  • 📊
    Usage reporting — know how many members are engaging, which modules are most popular, and where people are getting stuck. (Partner and Enterprise tiers.)
  • 📋
    Staff training guide included — a simple guide helps your staff introduce the programme to members and answer basic questions confidently.
