The DLEP Programme Ended March 2025

The federal Digital Literacy and Emergency Preparedness Programme — $47M over two phases — closed on March 31, 2025. Organisations that relied on DLEP-funded curriculum from Gluu, Connected Canadians, and HelpAge Canada no longer have a guaranteed supply of free structured digital literacy programming.

DCC fills that gap directly. Unlike DLEP-funded programmes, DCC does not depend on government grants to exist. A simple annual licence gives your organisation reliable, always-on curriculum that won't disappear when a programme authority ends.

NHSP-ready: Libraries and senior centres applying for New Horizons for Seniors Programme grants (up to $50,000 per project, 2026–27) can cite DCC as the curriculum platform. Your licence cost is covered within a typical NHSP grant budget. Ask us for an NHSP-ready letter of support →

Built-in outcome reporting: DCC now includes a knowledge check framework — before-and-after quizzes on core modules. After a workshop session, a facilitator opens one page and downloads a CSV report showing knowledge gains and confidence scores across the group. That report is the documented outcome evidence federal grant applications require. Your library leaves every DCC session with data you can use in your own funding applications.

📋 Using DCC in Your NHSP Grant Application

The New Horizons for Seniors Programme (NHSP) provides grants of up to $50,000 per project for activities that improve the lives of Canadian seniors. Digital literacy is an explicitly funded category. Many libraries and senior centres are applying right now for 2026–27 funding.

DCC fits NHSP criteria directly. Here is language you can use in your application:

Sample application language — copy and adapt:

"This project will deliver structured digital literacy training to seniors at [Organisation Name] using the Digital Confidence Centre (DCC), a free, AODA-accessible, bilingual (English/French) programme comprising 29 self-paced learning modules and a supporting library of plain-language guides for Canadian government services. DCC is designed specifically for seniors aged 65+ and is built to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards."

"All content is Canadian-specific and covers skills directly relevant to independent living: online safety, digital banking, government benefit portals (CRA My Account, My Service Canada Account, ServiceOntario), health technology, and staying connected with family."

"The total programme cost to the organisation is $[500/1,000] per year for a Partner/Multi-Site licence — well within the NHSP per-project budget. The remainder of grant funds will cover facilitator coordination, materials, and outreach to isolated seniors in our service area."

We can provide:

  • A written letter of support from Two Birds Innovation confirming your organisation's status as a DCC Partner and confirming the programme's eligibility as NHSP-qualifying digital literacy content
  • Programme statistics (number of modules, learners served, accessibility compliance documentation) for your application package
  • A designated contact name for the NHSP application reviewer

NHSP 2026–27 applications are typically accepted on a rolling basis. The sooner you apply, the better your chances. Contact us to request your letter of support →
