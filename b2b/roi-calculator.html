How we calculate this

Our estimates are based on research into digital literacy outcomes in Canadian communities, conversations with partner organisations, and data from similar programmes in Ontario. They are conservative estimates intended to give a general sense of value — not a guarantee.

Metric Formula Rationale
Members who benefit Total members × 0.35 Approximately 35% of Canadians aged 50+ report low digital confidence (Statistics Canada, 2023).
Value of literacy improvement Benefiting members × $180 Based on estimated value of reduced scam vulnerability, improved health service access, and reduced isolation — $180/year is a conservative proxy.
Hours saved Weekly hours × 52 × 0.40 Partner organisations report a 30–50% reduction in informal tech support queries after members complete DCC modules.
Staff cost saved Hours saved × hourly rate Direct labour cost of tech support time redirected to higher-value activities.
Cost per member $299 ÷ benefiting members Partner plan annual fee divided across the members who would actively benefit.
