Help Margaret spot the scam

Margaret is a pretend friend — she isn't real, and neither are her messages. She's just received a text and isn't sure about it. Sit beside her and help her decide. Helping someone else is the fastest way to build your own confidence.

Practice 2 of 3 — Find it for her

📥 Margaret's inbox — one of these is a scam

Margaret opens her email and asks, "Are any of these ones bad?" This inbox is make-believe. Nothing here is real and nothing can break. Two are ordinary emails. One is a scam. Tap the one you'd warn her about.
