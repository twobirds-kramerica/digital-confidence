Helping a parent get confident online?

You set up the iPad. You field the calls. Here is a resource built so your mum or dad can do this themselves, and so you can stop being the tech-support line.

  • ✓ No account to create
  • ✓ No tracking
  • ✓ Nothing to buy, ever
  • ✓ Canadian, plain language

Why is it free? This is a community initiative by Two Birds Innovation, an Ontario company, to help Canadian seniors stay safe and connected online. No fees, no ads, no sales calls, and nothing on any page can harm their device.

Send them their first lesson

Every lesson works on its own. It opens in any browser, needs no sign-in, and starts with a line that removes the fear before it teaches anything. Pick one and text them the link.

  • Best first lesson 🪂

    The Escape Hatch

    The one skill that fixes most tech trouble: how to safely back out of anything on screen. It ends the panic that causes most support calls.

    ⏱ 10 minutes to feel in control
  • 🤝

    Practice: Spot the Scam

    A gentle, pretend exercise. They help "a friend" decide what to do about a suspicious text, which is the calmest way to build real confidence.

    ⏱ 5 minutes to a first win
  • 🏦

    Online banking, safely

    The safety habits that protect their money, so a worried call to you becomes a calm check they can do themselves.

    ⏱ 15 minutes to banking confidence
The move is

Open The Escape Hatch on your own phone first, so you know exactly what they will see. Then copy the link from that page and send it in a text. That is the whole setup. There is nothing for them to install and no account to manage.
