## Narration — English (en-CA)

> Have you ever had a moment like this? Your phone buzzes. It's a text saying a parcel is held, and you just need to pay a small fee. And something feels… off.
>
> That feeling? Trust it. It's often right.
>
> Here are three quick tells. One: it's unexpected. Two: it rushes you. Three: it wants money, or your details.
>
> Here's the good news — you don't have to tap anything to find out. In this short lesson, you'll learn how to check a message like this, calmly, and know for sure.

## Narration — Canadian French (fr-CA) — DRAFT, requires human FR review before use

> Avez-vous déjà vécu un moment comme celui-ci? Votre téléphone sonne. C'est un texto qui dit qu'un colis est retenu et qu'il suffit de payer de petits frais. Et quelque chose vous semble… bizarre.
>
> Ce sentiment? Fiez-vous à lui. Il a souvent raison.
>
> Voici trois indices rapides. Un : c'est inattendu. Deux : on vous presse. Trois : on veut de l'argent, ou vos renseignements.
>
> Et la bonne nouvelle : vous n'avez rien à toucher pour le savoir. Dans cette courte leçon, vous apprendrez à vérifier un tel message, calmement, et à en avoir le cœur net.

> **FR REVIEW FLAG:** human Canadian-French review required before render — check register (warm, respectful "vous"), naturalness, and that "texto / renseignements / colis" read as Canadian, not France-French. A clumsy FR line to a senior audience is a trust landmine (spec §2.4).

## Character direction (Margaret)
- Etched DCC style, consistent with the master reference art (once built).
- Emotive beats: glance-worry (sc.1) → uncertainty, thumb hovering (sc.3) → calm decision, sets phone down (sc.4) → lighter, reassured (sc.6). Never panicked, never foolish — she's competent, just cautious. Dignity first.

## Accessibility

## Accuracy note
- "Canada Post parcel-fee text" is a real, widely-documented Canadian smishing pattern — safe to depict. No fabricated statistics used. Uses a generic example, not a real short-code or real link.
