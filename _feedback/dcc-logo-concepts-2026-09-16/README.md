## Design Reasoning & Brand Guideline Alignment

### 1. DCC Adults Concepts (`adult-favicon-concept.svg`, `adult-logo-concept.svg`)
The DCC Adults concept set anchors on the core brand essence: "Calm. Capable. Dignified." (~65–75 primary audience). It preserves the established DCC light bulb contour family motif from `classic/assets/logos/dcc/dcc-logo.svg` while introducing Aaron's suggested adult visual cue: a clean, elegant pair of spectacles/reading glasses worked into the center of the mark in warm amber (`#E0A63A`). Set against deep trust blue (`#1D4E89`), the mark reads clearly at favicon scale (32x32 / 16x16) with minimal shape count and high contrast. The full logo lockup pairs the mark with the Source Sans 3 wordmark and tier identification.

### 2. DCC Kids Concepts (`kids-favicon-concept.svg`, `kids-logo-concept.svg`)
The DCC Kids concept set delivers on the brand guideline requirement for the youth tier: "same etched style, younger proportions, age cue e.g. ballcap or ponytail, a light junior touch, still calm/serious-friendly, NOT cartoonish-silly". It retains the exact same family light bulb silhouette and base lines as the adult mark in the full logo lockup, ensuring the two products read as part of the same system. The kid-coded age cue is a stylized sideways baseball cap in burnt orange (`#E8842C`) resting on top of the mark, paired with calm, friendly eye dots and a subtle smile arc on warm teal (`#2A7B6F`). The full logo lockup pairs the mark with "DCC KIDS" in brand blue and burnt orange, clearly distinguishing the youth tier while maintaining family alignment.
