Company Overview

Who They Are

iA Financial Group (Industrial Alliance) is one of Canada's largest insurance and wealth management companies. Founded in 1892 in Quebec City. Publicly traded on TSX (IAG). Over 10,000 employees across Canada and the US.

What They Do

  • Individual life and health insurance
  • Group insurance (employer benefit plans)
  • Savings and retirement products (RRSPs, RRIFs, annuities)
  • Wealth management and mutual funds
  • Dealer services and auto/mortgage financing

Headquarters & Scale

  • HQ: Quebec City, Quebec
  • $200B+ in assets under management
  • Serves 4+ million Canadians
  • Offices across all provinces + US operations
  • Strong presence in Ontario, Atlantic Canada

Recent Strategic Direction

  • Digital transformation of client experience
  • Expansion of group benefits business
  • Acquisitions in US market (iA Auto & Home)
  • Focus on advisor technology and digital tools
  • ESG commitments and responsible investing

Culture & Values (Stated)

  • People first — client-centred and community-focused
  • Integrity — long-term relationships over short-term gains
  • Agility — adapting quickly in a changing market
  • Excellence — quality in every client interaction

Your Angle Into iA

iA serves seniors, families, and working Canadians — exactly the audience you've spent 50 days building for. Digital literacy, trust, and accessible communication are iA values and your demonstrated skills.

Your Story — STAR Format

Tip: Lead with the result. "In 50 days with no coding background, I built a working digital product for Canadian seniors, submitted a grant application, and secured an interview at a company like iA Financial." Then walk them back through the story.
S
Situation
Twenty-year career in Senior Project Management, primarily in enterprise environments. In early 2026, made the decision to transition from employment to building a consultancy. Sole provider for family — this was not a comfortable career experiment, it was a necessity to execute.
T
Task
Build a profitable, demonstrable business from zero within 50 days — with no prior coding background — that could serve as both a standalone product and a portfolio for consultancy work. Target audience: Canadian seniors needing digital literacy support.
A
Action
Used AI-assisted development to build the Digital Confidence Centre — a full-stack educational web platform with 11 modules, a scam simulator, a final assessment with certificate, a French language toggle, and a standalone AI accountability tool (P3). Submitted an SBEC grant application. Applied to and secured this iA Financial interview within the same window.
R
Result
Live product deployed to GitHub Pages at no cost. Grant application submitted. Interview secured with a major Canadian financial institution within 50 days of starting. Demonstrated that 20 years of PM discipline, applied to a new toolset, produces results faster than traditional timelines.
Bridge to iA: "The seniors I built for are iA's clients. The trust, clarity, and patience required to serve that audience — I've been doing that in product form. At iA, I'd bring that same focus to your projects."

Questions to Ask Them

1
You've been growing your group benefits and digital advisory business — how is this team positioned to support that growth, and what does success look like in the first 90 days for this role?
2
iA has been expanding into the US market and investing heavily in digital transformation. How does that affect day-to-day work for this team — are you building new capability or evolving existing process?
3
What's the biggest challenge the team is navigating right now that the right person in this role could directly help solve?
4
iA's stated values include agility and people-first thinking — how do those show up in how projects are actually run here, from your experience on the team?
5
I come from a background of building for non-technical end users — seniors, in my most recent project. Is there opportunity in this role to bridge between technical teams and client-facing stakeholders who need plain-language communication?

Logistics Checklist

Research your interviewer on LinkedIn tonight — find common ground and any public work
Test video and audio if the interview is remote — join a test meeting beforehand
Prepare 3 printed CVs if the interview is in person
Log in or arrive 5 minutes early — not 1 minute, not 10
Water bottle and a notepad with your 5 questions written out
Review your STAR story one more time Thursday night — out loud, not just in your head
Confirm the interview format, location, and who you're meeting — check the original email
Sleep before 11pm Thursday — you're sharp, and you need to show up sharp
