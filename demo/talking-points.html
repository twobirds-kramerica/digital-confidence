Sales Presentation Guide

Structured talking points for a 25-minute discovery / demo meeting. Total time: ~25 min. Adapt as needed — this is a guide, not a script.

Opening

~2 min

What to Show

  • The homepage loading at digitalconfidencecentre.ca
  • The accessibility bar at the top (font size buttons, dark mode toggle)
  • The sidebar navigation (scroll through it briefly)
  • The language switcher — flip to French, then back

What to Say

"What you're seeing is a free, bilingual digital literacy platform built specifically for seniors. Every element — the font size controls, the dark mode, the simple navigation — was designed with 70+ year olds in mind. Notice there's no login, no account, nothing to set up. A senior can open this on their iPad and start learning in under 60 seconds."

Likely Objection
"Is this just another e-learning tool?"
Your Response
"No. Most e-learning was built for people who already know how to use technology. This was built for people who've never touched a tablet before. It starts at the absolute beginning — Module 1 is literally called 'Mastering the Escape Hatch,' because we teach people how to close a window before we teach them anything else."

Core Value Proposition

~5 min

What to Show

  • Navigate to Module 1 from the sidebar
  • Point out the lesson structure: short sections, plain language, large text
  • Show the module progress tracker (checkboxes)
  • Show the star rating at the bottom of the module

What to Say

"There are 17 self-paced modules. Bilingual — English and French. Works on iPad, iPhone, and any computer. No account, no login, no email required. We cover everything from basic device skills to online banking to understanding AI. Seniors can go at their own pace, come back later, and nothing is ever lost."

Likely Objection
"How do we know seniors will actually use it?"
Your Response
"Because we designed for their specific context. iPad screen. Large text. No jargon. Ontario phone numbers in the resource directory. When someone opens this and sees a helpline number for their region, or a tip about the exact scam that's been hitting their neighbourhood this month — it feels like something made for them. Because it was."

Key Stats to Mention

  • 17 modules covering the full digital literacy journey
  • Fully bilingual (EN/FR)
  • Works on any device — no app to install
  • 4 font size levels + dark mode + dyslexia-friendly font
  • Ontario-specific resources and phone numbers

What Makes It Different

  • No ads, no login walls, no paywalls
  • Built from first principles for 70+ users
  • Scam simulator with real Ontario scenarios
  • Partners can white-label with their own branding
  • Free for end users — your organisation sponsors access

Live Demo Walkthrough

~15 min
Module 1 — The Escape Hatch Show the step-by-step layout, the checkboxes, the recap summary. "This is the kind of reassurance that builds confidence. Every little win is acknowledged."
Accessibility Bar Increase font to XL, switch to dark mode. "Two clicks. No hunting through settings. This is accessibility that actually works for the people who need it."
Scam Simulator Open a scam scenario. Show the reveal and the explanation. "This is active learning. Not just reading about scams — practising how to spot them. These are real Ontario-specific scenarios updated monthly."
Module Quiz Show a quiz inside a module. "Immediate feedback, no judgment. They can retry as many times as they want. The goal is confidence, not a grade."
Final Quiz Open final-quiz.html briefly. "For organisations that want a completion certificate pathway — this is the final assessment covering all 17 modules."
Resources Page Show the resource directory. "Local helpline numbers, device guides, links to Ontario government services. Everything a senior might need is one place."
French Language Switch to French. "Full translation — not machine-translated filler. Every module, every menu item, every button."
White Label Demo If available, show the London Public Library demo. "This is what it looks like with your organisation's branding. We can configure this in 48 hours."

The Line That Lands

"Notice anything that's missing? Ads. Login walls. Cookie consent banners that take up half the screen. Paywalls. Pop-ups asking seniors to subscribe to a newsletter. None of that. This is what a digital literacy platform looks like when you build it for the user, not for the platform."

The Framing Line

"What would it cost your organisation to build something like this from scratch? Development, design, accessibility testing, bilingual translation, content writing, hosting, maintenance — you're looking at $200,000 to $400,000, minimum, and two years of work. We've done that work. You're licensing the result — and you can have it live with your branding in 48 hours."

Likely Objection
"We don't have budget for this right now."
Your Response
"Totally fair. A few things that might help: the ROI calculator on our site lets you model the staff time savings against the annual fee — for most organisations with even a modest tech support burden, it pays for itself. And we offer quarterly billing on the Professional tier if annual is a barrier. What does your budget cycle look like?"

The Close

~3 min

The Offer

"Here's what I'd like to propose: I can set up a white-label demo with your organisation's branding in 48 hours — at absolutely no cost and no commitment. You get to see exactly what it would look like for your members before you make any decision. Would that be useful?"

Next Steps (Cover These Explicitly)

  • Leave your contact card (physical or digital)
  • Follow up within 48 hours with a summary email
  • Offer to send the ROI calculator link for their records
  • Ask: "Who else needs to be in the room for a go/no-go decision?"
  • Book a second meeting before you leave if possible
Likely Objection
"We need to take this back to our board / executive team."
Your Response
"Of course. I'll send you a one-page summary and the ROI calculator link so you have something concrete to share. Would it be helpful if I joined a 15-minute call with your team to answer questions directly? I want to make sure this is as easy as possible to evaluate."

After the Meeting

Follow up within 48 hours. Send the summary email. Keep it short: three sentences about what you showed, a link to the ROI calculator, and a single clear ask (a second meeting or a white-label demo approval).
