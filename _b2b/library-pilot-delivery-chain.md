# DCC Library Pilot — Delivery Chain Runbook
**Owner:** Aaron Patzalek · Two Birds Innovation
**Last updated:** 2026-06-10 (T3-1)
**Purpose:** What happens operationally when a library director says yes. Use this on discovery calls so you never have to improvise the logistics.

---

## The Offer on the Table

A **6-week pilot** at **no cost** for the first three libraries (Founding Partner rate: CA$500/yr for Year 1 after the pilot, if they proceed — the pilot itself is free).

What the library gets:
- Full DCC platform, no login required for patrons (just share the URL)
- Bilingual (EN/FR), 31 modules, WCAG AA
- Facilitator guide (twobirds-kramerica.github.io/digital-confidence/facilitator-guide.html)
- One 30-minute setup call with Aaron
- 6-week usage summary at the end

What the library provides:
- One staff contact who will facilitate sessions
- 5–10 seniors to participate
- A weekly session time (even 45 minutes/week is enough)

### Week 1: Setup call (30 min)

**What to cover:**

1. Walk through the platform — start on the home page, show the module grid, demonstrate Module 1 ("Your First Smartphone").
2. Explain the facilitator guide: session format (60 min), pace (half a module per session is fine), and the "what will you try this week?" closing question.
3. Confirm their patron count (5–10 is ideal for a first cohort) and session frequency (weekly works well).
4. Confirm first session date.
5. Ask: are any of their patrons francophone? If yes, show the language toggle.

**Send after the call:**

- Direct link to facilitator-guide.html
- The Module 1 suggested path: Module 1 → Module 2 → Module 3 (Scam Awareness) — scam awareness is the one seniors cite as immediately useful
- The NHSP reference if they mentioned grant interest: [URL]/institutional.html
- A calendar invite for a 15-minute check-in at Week 3

---

### Weeks 2–5: Facilitated sessions

Aaron's role: available by email, no active involvement unless asked.

Suggested module sequence for a 6-week cohort:
- Week 1: Module 1 (Your First Smartphone) or the module closest to their immediate concern
- Week 2: Module 2 (Safe Browsing Basics) or Module 3 (Scam Awareness) — scams are high-engagement
- Week 3: Patron-chosen: ask them what they want to learn next
- Week 4: Module 5 or 6 (Banking / Shopping Online)
- Week 5: Module 13 (Social Media Safety) or patron choice
- Week 6: Free exploration + certificates (if they want to print them)

Week 3 check-in (15 min): ask how sessions are going, whether patrons are coming back, and whether there are any friction points.

---

### Week 6: Closing

1. Send the **6-week usage summary** (see below).
2. Ask: did any patrons complete a module quiz? (Optional — the quiz and certificate are available but not required.)
3. Ask the two questions that matter for a case study:
   - "What's one thing a patron told you they did differently after a session?"
   - "Would you recommend DCC to a colleague at another library?" (Yes/No + reason)

**If they want to continue:** present the Starter tier at CA$500/yr (or Founding Partner rate if still available). No pressure — the pilot is complete either way.

## Discovery Call Talking Points

**When asked: "What does the pilot look like?"**

> "It's six weeks. Your staff runs one session per week with five to ten seniors. I send you the facilitator guide — it's a printed document, 10 pages — and we have one setup call before you start. At week three I check in for fifteen minutes. At week six I send you a one-page summary of what happened. No installation, no accounts, no IT involvement. The platform is a URL. You share the URL."

**When asked: "What does success look like?"**

> "At six weeks, I look for two things: did patrons come back for more than one session — that's the real signal — and did a patron do something differently, even one thing. Booked a medical appointment online. Logged in to their bank for the first time. Forwarded a suspicious email to family instead of clicking. One change in behaviour per cohort is a successful pilot."

**When asked: "What does it cost after the pilot?"**

> "The Starter tier is five hundred dollars a year. That's the DCC platform and the facilitator guide for unlimited patrons. If you are one of the first three libraries, you pay the Starter rate regardless of which tier you eventually want — that's the founding partner offer. Three spots. I have had one library confirm. Two remain."

**When asked: "Do I need my IT department?"**

> "No. It's a URL. twobirds-kramerica.github.io/digital-confidence/ — you can open it on any device, in any browser, right now. No login, no cookies, no data collection from patrons. If your IT department asks, the privacy policy is at that URL under the Privacy link."

## Notes

- The facilitator guide (facilitator-guide.html) covers session format, module sequence, pacing, and the closing question. It is the primary training document — no separate staff onboarding doc needed.
- Module 1 is not always the right starting point. If a senior's urgent question is "how do I not get scammed," start with Module 3. Ask the facilitator at the setup call.
- Do not send the NHSP institutional page unless the library has mentioned grants. It is useful; it is also a detour from the main ask.
- The 6-week framing is flexible. If a library runs biweekly sessions, a 10-week pilot is equivalent. The metric is sessions held, not calendar weeks.
