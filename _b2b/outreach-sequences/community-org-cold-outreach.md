## Email 2 — Day 4: The Isolation Angle
**Subject:** Loneliness and technology — what the research shows
**Preview text:** Why digital literacy is now a social determinant of health.

---

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note this week.

A National Institute on Ageing report from 2024 identified social isolation as the most significant health risk facing Canadian seniors over 75 — more impactful than physical inactivity, and contributing to cognitive decline, depression, and increased emergency health utilisation.

Technology, when seniors have the confidence to use it, demonstrably reduces isolation: regular video calls with family, online communities, and digital connections to services all show positive health outcomes.

The barrier is almost never access to the technology. It's confidence. That's what the Digital Confidence Centre addresses — Module 18 specifically covers video calling, messaging, and finding community online.

I know [Organisation Name] sees this every day in the field. DCC is one more tool for your team to offer.

Aaron

## Email 3 — Day 8: Field Worker Angle

If your frontline workers could point clients to a specific module that addresses their situation — "This one covers video calling your family, it takes about 20 minutes, and you can do it at your own pace" — that becomes a gift they take into every visit.

DCC is designed to work without a facilitator. But it also works beautifully as a supported activity during a visit.

I'm happy to put together a one-page guide for your team on which modules address which client needs. Just say the word.
