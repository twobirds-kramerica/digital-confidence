# Email Sequence 1: Library Cold Outreach
**Target:** Public library digital programs manager (or CEO/Executive Director at smaller libraries)
**Goal:** Get a 20-minute conversation about DCC
**Cadence:** 5 emails over 21 days

---

## Email 1 — Day 1: Introduction
**Subject:** Free digital literacy resource for your senior patrons
**Preview text:** Built for Ontario seniors — no cost, no setup, no catch.

---

Hi [First Name],

I'm Aaron Patzalek, a product manager from St. Thomas. I've spent the last few months building the Digital Confidence Centre — a free, bilingual digital literacy platform for Canadian seniors.

It's live now at [digital-confidence link]. 19 modules covering everything from phone safety to video calling to understanding AI. All accessible, all iPad-optimised, all free.

I'm reaching out because libraries are doing the most meaningful work around senior digital inclusion in this province — and I'd like to support that work, not compete with it.

Would it be useful to take a look?

Aaron Patzalek
Two Birds Innovation | St. Thomas, Ontario

## Email 2 — Day 4: Stat + Value
**Subject:** 1 in 3 Ontario seniors feels left behind by technology
**Preview text:** What that means for your library's digital programming.

---

Hi [First Name],

A quick follow-up to my note on Tuesday.

Statistics Canada's 2023 data shows that approximately 34% of Canadians over 65 report feeling uncomfortable using technology for everyday tasks — and the number rises sharply for those over 75.

For libraries, that's a significant portion of your community who may be reluctant to use self-checkout kiosks, online holds systems, or digital resources — not because they don't want to, but because no one has helped them build confidence.

The Digital Confidence Centre was built to close that gap — one patient, step-by-step module at a time.

Happy to send more detail whenever you're ready.

Aaron

---
**Personalisation notes:** Look up your target library's catchment area population and approximate senior demographics if possible — makes this email more specific.
**Send time:** 3 days after Email 1
**CTA:** Soft — "whenever you're ready"

---

## Email 3 — Day 8: The Brenda Story
**Subject:** What Brenda taught me about digital confidence
**Preview text:** A real story about a senior and her iPad.

---

Hi [First Name],

One more thought, if you'll allow it.

When I was designing the Digital Confidence Centre, I created a persona called Brenda. She's 74, lives alone in Southwestern Ontario, and her daughter moved to Vancouver two years ago. She has an iPad her kids bought her. She's been too nervous to use it properly since.

Brenda isn't fictional — she's a composite of dozens of seniors who filled out my early surveys. Her biggest fear wasn't getting hacked. It was pressing the wrong thing and breaking something. Her second biggest fear was looking foolish asking for help.

Every module I built starts with: "You are in a safe place. Nothing on this page can harm your device. You cannot break anything by reading."

That's what your senior patrons need too — permission to try.

The Digital Confidence Centre is designed to be used independently, in a workshop setting, or as a complement to your existing digital help programming.

Aaron

---
**Personalisation notes:** This email works as-is. If you know the library has a digital help desk or "ask a librarian" tech program, add: "It's also a great reference to give patrons when they leave your help desk."
**Send time:** 4 days after Email 2
