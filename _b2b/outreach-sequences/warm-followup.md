# Email Sequence 4: Warm Follow-up
**Use when:** Someone has responded to cold outreach, attended a demo, or expressed interest
**Goal:** Move from interested to committed
**Cadence:** 3 emails

---

## Email 1 — After Initial Response: Send Proposal
**Subject:** As promised — the DCC overview
**Preview text:** Everything you need to share with your team.

---

Hi [First Name],

Great to connect. As promised, here's what I'd like to share:

**The Digital Confidence Centre — Overview**
- Live URL: [DCC URL]
- 19 modules covering foundational digital skills for seniors
- Bilingual (English/French), WCAG 2.1 AA accessible
- iPad-optimised — designed for the devices most seniors actually use
- Free — no cost for patrons/members/clients to use

**How organisations are using it:**
- Direct referral: giving clients/members a specific module link that addresses their situation
- Workshop complement: using modules as pre/post workshop material
- Facilitated sessions: a staff member guides a small group through 1-2 modules together
- White-label licensing: organisations wishing to brand the platform for their community (contact for pricing)

**What I'd like to explore with you:**
[Customise based on the conversation — e.g., "I'd love to understand how your digital help sessions currently work and where DCC could fit." OR "I'd like to discuss what a pilot with your senior members might look like."]

Happy to answer any questions. What would be most useful as a next step?

Aaron Patzalek
[Two Birds contact — see private vault] | St. Thomas, Ontario

---
**Personalisation notes:** Always tailor the "What I'd like to explore" section to what came up in the first conversation.

---

## Email 2 — 5-7 Days After Proposal: Follow-up
**Subject:** Any questions on my end?
**Preview text:** Just checking in — happy to clarify anything.

---

Hi [First Name],

Just wanted to follow up on the overview I sent last week.

If any questions came up — about how the platform works, how other organisations are using it, or what a next step might look like — I'm happy to answer them.

If the timing isn't right, that's completely fine too. The resource is free and will be there whenever it's useful.

Aaron

---
**Send time:** 5-7 days after Email 1
**Keep it short** — don't re-sell. Just check in.

---

## Email 3 — After Demo or Pilot Agreement: Post-Demo Follow-up
**Subject:** Thank you for your time — next steps
**Preview text:** A quick summary of what we discussed.

---

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for taking the time [today / this week] — it was a genuinely useful conversation.

To summarise what we discussed:
- [Summary point 1 — e.g., "You'd like to pilot DCC with your Thursday digital help group starting in April"]
- [Summary point 2 — e.g., "Your team will need a one-page facilitator guide — I'll have that to you by [date]"]
- [Summary point 3 — e.g., "We'll reconnect in [month] to review how the pilot went"]

Is that an accurate summary? If I've missed anything, please let me know.

Looking forward to working together.

Aaron Patzalek
[Two Birds contact — see private vault]

---
**Personalisation notes:** Always write this email within 24 hours of the conversation while the details are fresh. Fill in the summary bullets with what was actually discussed.
