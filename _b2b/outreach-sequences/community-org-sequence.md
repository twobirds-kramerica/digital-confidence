## Email 2 — Day 6: The Isolation and Connection Angle
**Subject:** Technology shouldn't make seniors feel more alone — this helps
**Preview text:** Module 18 was built specifically for this.

---

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note last week.

Social isolation is one of the most significant health risks for older adults in Canada. And increasingly, the barrier isn't geography — it's digital confidence. Seniors who don't trust themselves to use video calling, messaging apps, or social media are cut off from the very tools their families use to stay in touch.

**Module 18: Staying Connected** in the Digital Confidence Centre was built specifically for this. It covers:
- How to make and receive FaceTime, Zoom, and WhatsApp video calls
- How to share photos with family through iCloud shared albums
- How to send voice messages and use emoji reactions
- How to find online communities and groups

This module was written for Canadian seniors who are starting from zero. It's free, self-paced, and works on an iPad, iPhone, or computer.

[DCC URL — direct link to Module 18]

If your organisation works with participants who've become more isolated since moving or losing a partner — this module was built for them.

Aaron

## Email 3 — Day 11: The Scam Vulnerability Angle
**Subject:** Your most vulnerable participants are being targeted online
**Preview text:** Scam protection, written at their level.

---

Hi [First Name],

One more thought.

Seniors living with fixed incomes, cognitive changes, or social isolation are disproportionately targeted by online and phone scammers. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre reports that Canadians over 60 lose more to fraud than any other age group — and most of those scams now begin digitally.

**Module 2: The Security Shield** in the Digital Confidence Centre addresses this directly in plain language:
- How to recognise a phishing email or fraud phone call
- The 3-Second Rule — pause before you click or respond to anything
- How to verify whether a message is really from who it claims to be
- What to do if you think you've been targeted

Many organisations serving seniors have found this module useful for group sessions — it works well projected on a screen and discussed together. It takes about 30 minutes and requires no login.

[DCC URL — Module 2]

Aaron
