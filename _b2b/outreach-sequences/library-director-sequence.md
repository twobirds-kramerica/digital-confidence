## Email 2 — Day 4: The Scam Protection Angle
**Subject:** 34% of Ontario seniors feel unsafe online — this is what helps
**Preview text:** The module your patrons need most is free and ready.

---

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note earlier this week.

A 2024 Digital Main Street survey found that 34% of Canadians over 65 report feeling uncomfortable using technology for everyday tasks — not because they lack the devices, but because they don't trust themselves to stay safe online.

For your senior patrons, this means real consequences: missed medical appointments booked online, vulnerability to phone and email scams, isolation from family who communicate digitally.

**Module 2: The Security Shield** in the Digital Confidence Centre addresses this directly. It covers:
- How to recognise phishing emails and fraudulent phone calls
- The 3-Second Rule (pause before you click or respond to anything)
- How to verify whether a call or email is legitimate
- What to do if you think you've been scammed

This module alone is worth sharing with your patrons. It's free, it works on any device, and it takes about 30 minutes to complete independently.

[DCC URL — direct link to Module 2]

Aaron

## Email 3 — Day 8: Brenda's Story
**Subject:** What it looks like when a senior finally feels confident online
**Preview text:** Meet Brenda — a composite of the people we built this for.

---

Hi [First Name],

One more thought.

Meet Brenda. She's 74, lives alone in St. Thomas, and her daughter moved to Vancouver two years ago. She has an iPad her son gave her for Christmas, but she's used it mostly to play solitaire. She's afraid of clicking the wrong thing. She's missed video calls with her grandchildren because she didn't know how to answer them.

Brenda isn't a real person — but she represents the patron who walks into your library, hesitates at the help desk, and asks "Is there someone who could just show me how to use this thing?"

The Digital Confidence Centre was built for Brenda. Every module starts from zero. Every instruction assumes no prior knowledge. Every warning is written in plain language, not tech jargon.

When your staff points Brenda to DCC, she can work through it at home, at her own pace, as many times as she needs. And Module 18 — on video calling and staying connected — might be what finally gets her talking to her grandchildren in Vancouver.

Aaron
