# Email Sequence 6: Grant Application Introduction
**Use for:** New Horizons for Seniors, Ontario Trillium Foundation, and other funders
**Goal:** Establish contact before submitting; get guidance on eligibility and fit
**Cadence:** 3 emails

---

## Email 1 — Initial Inquiry (Before Submitting)
**Subject:** Inquiry — [Grant Name] eligibility for digital literacy platform
**Preview text:** Request for guidance before formal application.

---

Dear [Program Officer Name / Program Team],

I am writing to inquire about eligibility before submitting a formal application to the [Grant Name] program.

**Organisation:** Two Birds Innovation, St. Thomas, Ontario
**Contact:** Aaron Patzalek | [Two Birds contact — see private vault]

**Project summary:** The Digital Confidence Centre is a free, bilingual digital literacy platform for Canadian seniors, currently serving learners across Southwestern Ontario. The platform provides 19 self-paced modules covering foundational digital skills — from device safety and online banking to video calling and AI literacy. It is WCAG 2.1 AA accessible and available in both English and French.

**Grant purpose request:** [Customise based on grant — e.g., "We are seeking $15,000 to support community outreach, workshop facilitation, and content development for the 2026-2027 programme year."]

**Primary question:** Given that Two Birds Innovation is a sole-proprietorship at this stage of development, I want to confirm whether our organisation structure is eligible before investing significant time in a formal application.

I would welcome a brief conversation or any written guidance you can provide.

Thank you,
Aaron Patzalek
Two Birds Innovation | St. Thomas, Ontario
[Two Birds contact — see private vault] | [Phone]

---
**Personalisation notes:**
- Check each grant's eligibility requirements carefully before sending — some require non-profit status, others accept social enterprises or small businesses
- For New Horizons: federal program, contact your regional Service Canada office
- For Trillium: contact the regional program manager at OTF

---

## Email 2 — Follow-up After Inquiry (7-10 days later)
**Subject:** Following up — [Grant Name] eligibility inquiry [Your Name / Org]
**Preview text:** Gentle follow-up on my inquiry last week.

---

Dear [Program Officer Name],

I wanted to follow up on my inquiry from [date] regarding eligibility for the [Grant Name] program.

If it would be more appropriate to proceed directly with a formal application rather than a pre-inquiry, I'm happy to do so. I wanted to avoid taking up your team's review time on an application that might not meet eligibility criteria.

I appreciate any guidance you're able to provide.

Aaron Patzalek
Two Birds Innovation | [Two Birds contact — see private vault]

---
**Send time:** 7-10 days after Email 1
**Keep it very short** — program officers are busy and appreciate brevity.

---

## Email 3 — Post-Application Thank You
**Subject:** Thank you — [Grant Name] application submitted
**Preview text:** Application reference and follow-up offer.

---

Dear [Program Officer Name],

I wanted to let you know that I submitted our formal application to the [Grant Name] program on [date]. The application reference number is [if provided].

Thank you for [any guidance you provided in your response / taking the time to respond / the clear instructions on the program portal].

If any additional information or clarification would be useful during the review process, I'm happy to provide it.

Aaron Patzalek
Two Birds Innovation | [Two Birds contact — see private vault]

---
**Personalisation notes:**
- Only send this if you had a prior exchange with the program officer
- This email serves two purposes: closes the loop professionally, and ensures the officer remembers a real person is behind the application
- Keep it brief — no selling, no summarising the application
