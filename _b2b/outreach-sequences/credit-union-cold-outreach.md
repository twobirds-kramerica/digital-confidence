**Cadence:** 5 emails over 21 days

## Email 2 — Day 4: Financial Safety Angle
**Subject:** $556M lost to fraud in Canada last year — most victims are seniors
**Preview text:** What your credit union can do about it.

---

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note this week.

The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre reported over $556 million in fraud losses in Canada in 2023 — and seniors are disproportionately targeted. Not because they're naive, but because they're often the primary holders of savings, and because financial scams have become increasingly sophisticated.

The most effective defence isn't software. It's education. A member who knows how to recognise a phishing email, how to verify a caller's identity, and what to do in the first 10 minutes after a suspected fraud — that member is dramatically safer.

The Digital Confidence Centre's Module 2 (Security Shield) and Module 5 (Email & Messages) are built specifically around these skills. Module 6 covers banking and transaction safety.

These are free resources your members could access today — at home, on their iPad, at their own pace.

Aaron

---
**Personalisation notes:** Replace the fraud statistics with the most current data you can find from CAFC. If the credit union has communicated about fraud prevention before, reference that: "I saw [Credit Union Name] shared fraud warnings in your newsletter — DCC goes deeper on all of those topics."

## Email 3 — Day 8: Trust and Community Angle
**Subject:** The reason your senior members trust you — and how DCC supports that
**Preview text:** Connection, not just protection.

---

Hi [First Name],

Credit unions are different from banks. Your members know it. Your staff knows it.

That difference — the relationship, the community rootedness, the genuine care for member wellbeing — is exactly what makes digital literacy programming a natural extension of your mission.

When a senior member learns something at their credit union, they trust it. When their credit union recommends a resource, they use it.

The Digital Confidence Centre was built to be that kind of trusted resource. Not a product to sell them. A programme that says: we want you to feel confident and safe using the tools that matter in your life.

I'd love to explore whether there's a fit here — either as a resource to share with members, or as a component of a workshop programme your branch staff could facilitate.

Aaron

---
**Personalisation notes:** This email works for any credit union. If you know the credit union runs financial literacy workshops or community events, mention it.
