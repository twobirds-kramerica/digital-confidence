# Email Sequence: Warm Follow-Up (After Positive Signal)
**Trigger:** Contact has opened multiple emails, clicked a link, replied with interest but no meeting booked, or said "send me more info"
**Goal:** Convert interest into a 20-minute discovery call
**Cadence:** 3 emails over 10 days

---

## Email 1 — Day 1: Acknowledge the Signal
**Subject:** Re: Digital Confidence Centre — happy to tell you more
**Preview text:** You showed interest. Here's the short version of what a partnership could look like.

---

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for [opening the emails / clicking through to the site / getting back to me — use whichever is accurate].

I want to give you a clear picture of what a partnership with the Digital Confidence Centre actually looks like — without a lot of sales language.

**What DCC is:**
A free, bilingual digital literacy platform for Canadian seniors. 19 modules. WCAG 2.1 AA. Works on any device. No registration required.

**What a partnership looks like:**
At the basic level — you point your participants/patrons/members to the free site. That's it. Nothing to install, nothing to budget for.

For organisations that want more, there's a **white-label option**: DCC content branded with your organisation's name and logo, hosted at a subdomain you control, with optional custom intake and reporting. This is a paid arrangement and we'd scope it based on your needs.

**What I'm asking for right now:**
A 20-minute call. That's it. No pitch deck. Just a conversation about whether DCC fits what you're already trying to do for your seniors.

Would [Day], [Day], or [Day] work? Happy to send a calendar link if easier.

Aaron Patzalek
Two Birds Innovation | St. Thomas, Ontario
[Two Birds contact — see private vault]

---
**Personalisation notes:**
- Reference specifically what they clicked or said if known ("I saw you visited the Module 2 scam protection page — happy to walk through that content with you")
- Keep the tone low-pressure — they showed interest, don't oversell

---

## Email 2 — Day 5: Remove the Friction
**Subject:** If a call feels like too much — here's the no-meeting option
**Preview text:** No calendar required. Just a direct question for you.

---

Hi [First Name],

I know a 20-minute call is a commitment, even a small one.

If it's easier, just reply to this email with one of these:

**A)** Yes, I'd like to set up a call — [send me your calendar link / I'm available X days]

**B)** We'd like to share the free DCC link with our participants — no partnership needed right now

**C)** This isn't a fit for us — thanks for reaching out

Any of those is a fine answer. I just want to make sure you have what you need.

The free resource is always available at [DCC URL] — your participants can access it today regardless of what you decide.

Aaron

---

## Email 3 — Day 10: Final Warm Follow-Up
**Subject:** Last check-in — then I'll leave you to it
**Preview text:** One last nudge, then I'm out of your inbox.

---

Hi [First Name],

Last note from me on this.

If now isn't the right time for a partnership conversation — completely understood. Timing matters.

What I'd love to leave you with:

1. **The free resource link**: [DCC URL] — share it with your participants anytime, no strings attached
2. **My contact**: [Two Birds contact — see private vault] — if the timing changes, or if a grant comes up where a digital literacy partner would be useful, I'm here
3. **One specific module worth bookmarking**: Module 2 — scam protection — [module-2 URL] — it's the one your seniors need most and it's 30 minutes, free, no login

Thanks for your time. Keep doing good work.

Aaron Patzalek
Two Birds Innovation | St. Thomas, Ontario

---
**Send time:** 10 days after Email 1 in this sequence
**Decision rule:** After this email, move to "passive nurture" — add to a low-frequency list (quarterly check-in maximum). No individual follow-up unless they respond or a triggering event occurs.
