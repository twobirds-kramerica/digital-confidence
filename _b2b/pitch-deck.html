The Problem

Ontario seniors are increasingly expected to manage their lives online — banking, healthcare appointments, government services, communication with family. Yet 42% of Canadians aged 65+ report low confidence with digital technology. Libraries, municipalities, and community organisations are asked to bridge this gap — often without the content, tools, or budget to do so.

The Solution

The Digital Confidence Centre is a complete, ready-to-deploy digital literacy curriculum designed specifically for Canadian seniors aged 65+. It covers everything from "how to close a scary pop-up" to "how to negotiate your internet bill" — in plain language, with bilingual English/French support, and accessibility built into every page.

Platform Features

Trust-First Design

Module 1 teaches how to leave before anything else. Anxiety comes first, then empowerment. Seniors feel safe from the start.

Bilingual EN/FR

Every module, every button, every help text is available in both English and French with a one-tap language toggle.

Accessibility (WCAG AA)

4-level font sizing, dyslexia-friendly fonts, dark mode, high contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatible.

Quiz System

5-question quiz per module with immediate feedback. Final comprehensive assessment with printable certificate.

Progress Tracking

Visual progress indicators, completion celebrations, personalised recommendations based on learning history.

Zero IT Overhead

Static HTML — no servers, no databases, no maintenance. Hosted on your existing website or ours. Works on any device.

Scam Awareness

60+ scam scenarios in an interactive simulator. Real-world examples from the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.

Print-Friendly

Cheat sheets, quick reference cards, and full module content all print-ready for seniors who prefer paper.
