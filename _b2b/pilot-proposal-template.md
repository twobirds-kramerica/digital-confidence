## Why DCC

- **Built for seniors, not adapted for them.** Every design decision starts from "Would a 74-year-old iPad user understand this?"
- **Trust-first design.** Module 1 teaches how to leave before teaching anything else. Seniors feel safe from the start.
- **Canadian content.** Ontario-specific resources, Canadian phone numbers, CRTC data, CAFC scam examples.
- **Zero IT overhead.** Static HTML files — no servers, no databases, no software to install or maintain.
- **Proven platform.** 241 pages, live since March 2026, built by a 20-year Senior Product Manager.
