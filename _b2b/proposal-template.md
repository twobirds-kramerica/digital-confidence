## The Digital Confidence Centre

Digital Confidence Centre is an online learning platform built specifically for older adults in Ontario. It teaches smartphone safety, scam recognition, online banking, video calling, and more — all in plain language, at a pace that works for people who didn't grow up with this technology.

The platform includes:

- **15 self-paced learning modules** covering the digital skills older adults need most
- **Quick Answers** — a searchable resource library for common questions
- **Scam Awareness hub** — Ontario-specific scam alerts and deep-dive guides
- **Bilingual content** — fully available in English and French
- **Accessibility-first design** — high contrast, adjustable text size, iPad-optimised

The platform is free for all Ontario residents to access directly. Your organisation would offer it under your own brand as a valued service for your [patrons / members / clients].
