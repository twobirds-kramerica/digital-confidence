## Template 1 — Initial Cold Email: Library Director

**Subject:** Digital literacy programme for older adults — partnership opportunity

**Timing:** Send Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11 AM. Personalise before sending.

**Personalisation notes:**
- Replace `[Library Name]` and `[Director Name]` throughout
- Look up 1 recent initiative from their website (new branch, award, programme) and reference it in the opener
- Check their current digital literacy offerings — adjust paragraph 3 if they already have something running

---

Hi [Director Name],

I came across [Library's recent initiative / award / programme] — congratulations on that. It's clear [Library Name] takes community programming seriously.

I'm reaching out because we've built something that fits naturally alongside that work. Digital Confidence Centre is an online learning platform designed specifically for older adults — it covers smartphone safety, scam recognition, online banking, and more, all in plain language built for people who didn't grow up with this technology.

We license it to libraries, credit unions, and community organisations across Ontario as a white-label product. Your branch would offer it under your own brand, with no technical setup required on your end. It's fully accessible, tested with adults 70+, and works on any device.

Given what you're already doing with community programming, I think this could be a strong fit. Would you be open to a 20-minute call to see if it makes sense for [Library Name]?

Happy to share a live demo link in advance so you can explore it before we talk.
