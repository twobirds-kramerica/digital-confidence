How these activities work

🧑‍🤝‍🧑
Do it together. Every activity is designed for one caregiver and one child. No solo screen time required.
⏱
10 minutes each. Short enough for a library programme or a quiet evening. Long enough to make it stick.
📋
Print or read aloud. Each page works on a phone, a tablet, or printed. No special materials needed.
🔬
Research-backed. Every activity cites its developmental and media literacy sources in a caregiver section at the bottom.
