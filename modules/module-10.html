🛒 Everyday errands

Grocery and Food Delivery Apps

⏱ About 20 minutes, at your own pace

✅ You are in a safe place. Nothing on this page can harm your device.

How to order groceries and restaurant meals delivered right to your door, safely, simply, and at a price you can see before you pay.

Linda's story

Linda, 78, has mild arthritis in her knees. She still lives independently and loves to cook, but the weekly grocery run had become exhausting: the parking lot walk, pushing a heavy cart, loading bags into the car, then carrying them up the front steps.

Her daughter suggested trying grocery delivery. Linda was nervous. "What if they pick rotten produce? What if someone steals my credit card number? What if I order the wrong thing and cannot get a refund?"

Her daughter helped her place the first order. The shopper sent a photo asking "Is this the right size of yogurt?", which made Linda laugh. Everything arrived in an hour, exactly as ordered. The produce was perfect. The bags were left neatly at her door.

Now Linda uses grocery delivery every week. She spends the time she saves on gardening, reading, and her granddaughter's soccer games. "It has genuinely changed my life," she says. "I do not know why I was so worried."

From your coach

Ordering groceries online for the first time can feel like trusting a stranger with your shopping list. It is normal to worry about wrong orders or hidden fees. Remember: you can browse every aisle of these apps without buying a single thing, for as long as you like. Looking costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Before we start

Delivery apps are completely safe when used from their official sources (the App Store or Google Play). Your payment information is encrypted, as secure as shopping online at any major Canadian retailer. You will see the full cost before you confirm any order.

Part 1: Grocery delivery apps

Instacart, the most popular in Ontario

What it is: Instacart partners with your local grocery stores (Loblaws, Metro, Costco, FreshCo, and more). A real person, called a personal shopper, goes to the store, picks your items, and delivers them to your door. You can often get same-day delivery.

What Instacart looks like: the Instacart app icon is a green shopping cart with an orange carrot. Find it in the Apple App Store (for iPhone or iPad) or the Google Play Store (for Android). It is free to download.

Step 1: Download and create an account

1

On iPhone or iPad: open the App Store (blue icon with a white "A"). Tap the Search tab at the bottom, type "Instacart", and tap GET to download. The icon is a green shopping cart with an orange carrot.
2

On Android: open the Google Play Store (colourful triangle icon). Tap the search bar at the top, type "Instacart", and tap Install.
3

On a computer: open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox) and go to instacart.ca. No app to download, you can use the website directly. Click Sign up in the top-right corner.
4

Tap Sign Up. Enter your email address and create a password. You will receive a verification email, tap the link inside it.
5

Add your payment method, a credit or debit card. The number is stored securely and encrypted. Instacart uses the same security as major Canadian banks.
6

Allow location access when prompted. This shows you which stores deliver to your neighbourhood. You can always change your address in Settings.

Step 2: Placing your first order

1

Choose your store: tap the store you normally shop at (for example "Loblaws" or "Metro"). You will see their full product catalogue.
2

Add items: use the search bar to find products (for example type "2% milk" or "whole wheat bread"). Or browse by category: Produce, Dairy, Bakery, and so on. Tap + Add to put items in your cart.
3

Review your cart: tap the cart icon. You will see your full order and the total cost, with the delivery fee, service fee, and suggested tip all shown separately, before you pay anything.
4

Choose delivery time: "As soon as possible" (usually 1 to 2 hours) or schedule for a specific time when you will be home.
5

Add special instructions: you can type notes like "Please knock loudly, I am hard of hearing" or "Leave groceries at the door." Your shopper will read these.
6

Place your order: review the final total one more time, then tap "Place Order." You will get text and app updates as your shopper selects your items and when they are on their way.
Cost Breakdown Example

Groceries: $75.00. Delivery fee: $3.99 (sometimes free for your first order). Service fee: $5.00. Suggested tip (15%): $11.25. Total: approximately $95. You see all of this before you confirm. There are no hidden surprises.

Safety and quality

Instacart shoppers are background-checked. If an item is wrong or missing, you can request a refund through the app in seconds, and Instacart's support is very responsive. You can also rate your shopper, which maintains quality.

Other grocery options

  • Walmart Grocery Pickup, free and easy: order online at walmart.ca, choose a pickup time, and drive to your local Walmart. Staff load your groceries into your car, you never need to go inside the store. No delivery fee. Available at most Walmart locations in Ontario.
  • PC Express (Loblaws family): order online from Loblaws, No Frills, or Real Canadian Superstore. Choose curbside pickup or home delivery. Your PC Optimum points still apply, you earn points on every order.

Part 2: Food delivery apps (restaurant meals)

Uber Eats, order from local restaurants

Getting started with Uber Eats

1

On iPhone or iPad: open the App Store, search "Uber Eats", and tap GET. The icon is dark with a fork and knife.
2

On Android: open the Google Play Store, search "Uber Eats", and tap Install.
3

On a computer: go to ubereats.com in your browser, no app needed. Click Sign in to create an account or log in.
4

Create an account with your email address, or log in if you already have an Uber account (the same login works for both).
5

Enter your delivery address. The app shows all restaurants that deliver to you, along with estimated delivery times and minimum order amounts.
6

Browse menus, add items to your cart, and review the full cost including delivery fee and service fee before confirming your order.
Understanding Uber Eats Fees

Delivery fee: based on distance, typically $2 to $8. Service fee: Uber's charge, usually 10 to 15% of your subtotal. Small order fee: added if your order is under $10, just order a little more. Surge pricing: fees go up during busy times (Friday dinner, bad weather), order earlier or later to save.

Other food delivery apps

  • DoorDash (red and white logo): delivery fee $1.99 to $5.99. Best for restaurant variety and DashPass members.
  • Skip the Dishes (Canadian): delivery fee $2.99 to $6.99. Best for Canadian chains (Tim Hortons, Swiss Chalet) and smaller cities.
  • Instacart (green and orange): delivery fee $3.99 to $7.99. Best for groceries and household items.
Canadian Pick

Skip the Dishes is a Canadian company and often has the best selection in smaller Ontario cities like St. Thomas and Woodstock. All these apps are available in Windsor, London, Kitchener, and surrounding areas.

The 3-second rule

  1. ✋Stop Do not tap anything yet.
  2. 🫁Breathe Take one slow breath.
  3. 🔍Verify Did I expect this message? Does the sender look right? When in doubt, close it and call someone you trust.

Your payment is safe

How Payments Work

All delivery apps use bank-level encryption for your payment information. Your credit or debit card number is never stored in plain text, it is converted into a secure token. This is the same technology used by major Canadian banks and retailers like Amazon and Hudson's Bay. You will always see the full cost before you confirm. You can also request a refund through the app if anything is wrong with your order.
