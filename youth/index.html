Digital Confidence Youth
For ages 13–17

Know the trick before it’s played.

Eight short modules on how apps, games, ads — and sometimes even strangers and friends — push people around online, and how to spot it happening. No lectures, no “screens are bad”, no telling you to quit anything.

  • Free
  • No account, no tracking
  • About 15 minutes each
  • Made in Canada

The modules

Each one is built around real situations, not rules. You pick what you’d do; the debrief tells you what’s actually going on.

  • New · Sextortion

    Blackmail Has a Playbook

    Someone threatening to share images — real or AI-faked — is running a known script. Every step of it, named, plus the counter-move that actually works. None of it is your fault.

    About 12 minutes
  • New · Algorithms

    Who Picked This For You?

    Your feed isn’t random and it isn’t magic. The signals that actually train it — watch time, not likes — why feeds narrow into rabbit holes, and the reset buttons the apps themselves built.

    About 12 minutes
  • Doomscrolling

    How Do You Feel After?

    You opened it to check one thing and lost 45 minutes. Why the feed never ends, why it serves you the upsetting stuff, and the one honest signal that beats counting minutes.

    About 12 minutes
  • AI companions

    The Friend Who Never Logs Off

    AI companion apps always agree, never get busy, and say they’ll miss you. What’s actually on the other side of that chat — and where your late-night messages really go.

    About 15 minutes
  • AI & deepfakes

    AI in Real Life

    Voice clones, a deepfake in the group chat, AI and schoolwork, and what AI is genuinely good for. Four situations you could actually hit this year.

    About 15 minutes
  • The attention economy

    Who’s Paying for Your Attention?

    If the app is free, someone else is paying — and what they’re buying is your attention. How feeds, countdown timers, and #sponsored posts really work.

    About 15 minutes
  • Games & spending

    Gems Are Real Money

    Loot boxes, grind walls, and item-shop timers. The design tricks that turn game currency into real spending — named, so you can see them coming.

    About 15 minutes
  • Friends & pressure

    The Pressure You Can Name

    Streaks, group pile-ons, and “if you really cared…”. What manipulation looks like inside a chat — and what naming it lets you do.

    About 15 minutes

If something online has gone past “annoying”

If someone is threatening you, pressuring you for photos, or you’re just carrying something heavy — that’s not a “figure it out yourself” situation, and asking for help isn’t weakness.

  • Kids Help Phone — call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868. Free, 24/7, confidential, anywhere in Canada.
  • Cybertip.ca — if someone has (or claims to have) intimate images of you or someone you know, report it at Cybertip.ca. It happens a lot, it’s not your fault, and there are real steps that work.
