## Proposed Solution

### The Digital Confidence Centre

A fully white-labeled version of the Digital Confidence Centre, branded as **"[CLIENT NAME] Digital Skills Centre"**, deployed at:

`[CLIENT-DOMAIN.com/digital-skills]` or `digital-skills.[client-domain].ca`

**Curriculum — 15 Modules:**

| Module | Topic | Relevance to [CLIENT] |
|--------|-------|----------------------|
| 1 | Digital Safety Basics | Foundation for all digital use |
| 2 | The Security Shield | Scam protection — reduces fraud claims |
| 3 | Passwords & Biometrics | Account security |
| 4 | App Store Safety | Mobile device protection |
| 5 | Email & Messages | Phishing defence |
| 6 | Online Banking | [CLIENT] app and portal usage |
| 7–12 | Daily Digital Life | Independence and confidence |
| 13 | Social Media Safety | Facebook/Instagram for seniors |
| 14 | Smart Home Basics | Connected device literacy |
| 15 | Telehealth & Medical Portals | Healthcare access |

**Technical Details:**
- Pure static HTML/CSS/JavaScript — no server required
- Hosted on GitHub Pages or [CLIENT]'s existing web infrastructure
- No user data collected — PIPEDA compliant
- Fully accessible: WCAG AA, 4-level font sizing, dark mode, dyslexia-friendly font
- Works on all devices: iPad, iPhone, laptop, desktop
