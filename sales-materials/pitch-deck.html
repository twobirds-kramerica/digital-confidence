The Challenge

Ontario's Seniors Are Being Left Behind Digitally

The Numbers

  • 67% of Ontario seniors (70+) report technology anxiety
  • $1.6 billion lost to elder fraud in Canada annually
  • Telehealth adoption: seniors miss appointments they can't navigate
  • OAS/GIS applications increasingly digital-only
  • Social isolation epidemic — digital exclusion accelerates it

The Business Impact

  • Fraud claims cost financial institutions millions annually
  • Digitally excluded members generate higher service costs
  • Member/patron satisfaction declining as digital-first expands
  • Libraries face reduced relevance as services move online
  • Community organisations lack credible digital literacy tools
