Beta Programme Instructions

1. Send testers the welcome email (see welcome-email.html for template).
2. Ask testers to use the Ideas & Feedback button on any page to submit thoughts.
3. After 1–2 weeks, send testers the Beta Survey link.
4. Download Formspree CSV weekly during the beta period.
5. Check Google Analytics weekly for module engagement data.
6. Log qualitative notes (phone calls, emails) in the notes field above.
7. After beta closes, compile findings and prioritise changes before public launch.
