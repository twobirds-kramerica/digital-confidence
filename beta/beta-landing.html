Help Us Make Digital Learning Better for Seniors Like You

We're looking for 20 Ontario seniors aged 65+ to try out our learning programme and tell us what they think.

What's Involved?

It's relaxed, friendly, and completely at your own pace. Here's what you can expect:

  • 📅
    3–4 weeks — the programme runs over about a month. You go at whatever speed feels right for you.
  • ⏱️
    2–3 hours per week — that's roughly 20–30 minutes a day, a few days a week. No pressure.
  • 💻
    No tech experience needed — this programme is designed for people who are just starting out. If you can tap a screen, you're ready.
  • 💬
    Share your honest thoughts — after each lesson, you'll have the option to leave a short comment. What was confusing? What helped? What was missing?
  • 🎁
    No cost, no obligation — this is entirely free. You can stop at any time, no explanation needed.

Why Does Your Opinion Matter?

Most technology is designed by young people, for young people. We're doing something different — building a programme with seniors, not just for them. Your feedback will shape what the final lessons look like. Your voice matters.
