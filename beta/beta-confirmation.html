You're In! Thank You So Much.

We've received your sign-up and we're genuinely excited to have you on board. Your experience and perspective are exactly what we need.

What Happens Next?

  1. 1
    We review your sign-up — we go through all applications and confirm your spot within 3 business days.
  2. 2
    You'll receive a welcome email — it will include your official welcome letter, the beta guide, and a link to begin.
  3. 3
    You start when you're ready — there's no fixed start date. Begin on your own schedule, at your own pace.
  4. 4
    Share your thoughts as you go — a blue feedback button on every page makes it easy to tell us what's working and what isn't.
  5. 5
    We improve the programme — your feedback directly shapes the final version that thousands of seniors will use.

Not sure what to expect? That's completely normal. We've written a friendly Beta Tester Guide that explains everything in plain language — what you'll do, how long it takes, and how to give feedback. You can read it any time.

You don't have to wait for our email. The learning centre is open right now — no password, no login, no registration needed.
