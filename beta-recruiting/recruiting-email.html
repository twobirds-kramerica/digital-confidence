I'm reaching out because [ORGANIZATION NAME] plays a vital role in supporting seniors across our community. I'd love your help connecting with a few of the older adults you serve — specifically to participate in the beta launch of Digital Confidence Centre, a free digital literacy programme designed for adults 70 and up.

We're not a classroom and we're not a manual. Digital Confidence Centre is a friendly, self-paced online programme that teaches seniors how to use their iPhone or iPad more safely — everything from avoiding scams to using apps with confidence. We built it for seniors who are capable and curious, but who haven't always had someone explain things in a way that actually makes sense.

We're looking to recruit 2–5 participants from your network to help us test the programme before public launch.

Why Participate?

  • Free access — no cost to your organisation or to participants, ever
  • Direct input — participants help shape a tool that will serve thousands of Ontario seniors
  • Real skills — participants will genuinely learn and gain confidence with their devices
  • Community contribution — a meaningful, low-commitment way for seniors to give back
  • No tech background required — we designed this specifically for beginners

What's Involved

⏱  Time commitment: approximately 2–3 hours total over 4 weeks

📅  Beta period: April 1 – May 15, 2026

📱  Device required: iPhone or iPad with internet access

  • Complete 2–3 short learning modules at their own pace (15–20 minutes each)
  • Fill in a simple feedback form after each module (5–10 minutes)
  • Optional: a relaxed 30-minute phone conversation with our team to share their thoughts
  • That's it — no apps to install, no passwords to remember, no in-person attendance required

Ideal Participants

We're looking for participants who:

  • Are 70 years of age or older
  • Own or have regular access to an iPhone or iPad
  • Have basic internet access at home or through your facility
  • Are willing to try something new — even if they feel unsure about technology
  • Are comfortable reading and responding to simple email or text messages
  • Live in Ontario (Elgin County, Middlesex County, or surrounding Southwestern Ontario preferred)

We especially welcome participants who are newer to smartphones or who have expressed interest in learning more about using technology safely.

I'm happy to provide printed flyers or a short description you can share in your newsletter or on your screens.

Interested participants can reach out to me directly, or you can collect names and I'll follow up personally. Whichever is easier for you.
