Full Transcript — Module 1: The Escape Hatch

Professional transcript coming when the video is produced. This is a placeholder. The transcript will appear here word-for-word so you can read along or refer back to any part of the video.

[0:00] Welcome to Module 1 of the Digital Confidence Centre. Today we are going to learn the single most important skill for using your iPad safely...

[1:15] The Home Button is the round button at the bottom of your iPad. On newer iPads, it is a swipe gesture from the bottom instead. No matter what is happening on your screen, pressing or swiping Home will always bring you back to safety...

[2:30] The Back Button appears in the top-left corner of most apps. It usually looks like a left-pointing arrow and has the name of the previous screen. Tapping it takes you one step back, just like the back button in a car...

[4:00] Now it is your turn to practise. Try opening the Settings app, then pressing Home to return to the Home Screen. You cannot break anything. Everything you see on your screen can be undone...
