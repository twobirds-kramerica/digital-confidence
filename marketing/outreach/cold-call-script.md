# Cold Call Script — Digital Confidence Centre
## Two Birds Innovation — B2B Sales Calls

---

## OPENING
*(2 sentences max — warm, direct, no wasted words)*

"Hi [First Name], my name is Aaron Kramer — I'm the founder of Two Birds Innovation, and I built a digital literacy platform specifically for credit unions in Ontario to help their senior members bank online with more confidence. Is now a reasonable time for two minutes?"

*[If yes, continue. If no, jump to objection handling below.]*

---

## QUALIFYING QUESTION

"Before I say anything else — what does [Credit Union / Library / Centre Name] currently offer for members or patrons who are over 70 and struggling with digital banking or smartphones? Is that something you're actively trying to solve, or is it more of a background concern right now?"

*[Listen carefully. Their answer tells you the sales angle: active problem vs. latent need.]*

---

## BRIDGE TO PITCH
*(30-second version)*

"That makes sense. What we built is called Digital Confidence Centre — it's a white-label platform that sits under your brand, and it walks adults 70+ through 16 modules: scam recognition, password safety, online banking, smartphone basics. The whole thing is written for seniors, not tech-savvy people.

The credit unions we're talking to are using it mainly because it reduces fraud-related claims and the callback volume that comes with it — and because their senior members actually feel like the credit union is looking out for them, which matters for retention.

The annual licence is $12,000 for a financial institution your size, and it's eligible to be offset through New Horizons for Seniors federal funding — so Year 1 can often be close to cost-neutral.

I'd love to show you the platform. It takes about 15 minutes and I can walk you through a demo site branded for a fictional credit union so you can see exactly what your members would see."

---

## ASK FOR THE MEETING

"Would you have 15 minutes this week or next to take a look? I can send you a calendar link or we can pick a time right now — whatever's easier."

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING

---

### "Not interested."

"Completely fair — I appreciate you being direct. Can I ask just one thing before I let you go: is it more that it's not a fit for your members, or that the timing isn't right? That helps me know whether it's worth following up later."

*[If timing: ask if Q3/Q4 is better. If not a fit: thank them and end the call gracefully.]*

---

### "We don't have time right now."

"Understood, things are busy. Would it be worth putting something in the calendar for three or four weeks out? I'm not trying to rush you — I just don't want you to lose the thread if it's something you'd want to revisit."

*[Offer a specific future date. If they decline, ask for permission to send an email instead.]*

---

### "Call me back later."

"Happy to do that. What's a better time — later this week, or would early next week work better? And is email a better way to confirm the time?"

*[Pin down a specific time. Vague "call me back later" without a date is a polite no — get something concrete or acknowledge the call is over.]*

---

### "We already have something for this."

"That's good to hear — it tells me your members' digital confidence is already on your radar. Can I ask what you're using? I want to understand whether what we've built is genuinely different or just more of the same."

*[Listen. If their current solution is workshops, YouTube videos, or generic content — those are all opening points. If they have a real competitor, acknowledge it and ask what's working and what isn't.]*

---

### "I need to talk to my team / management."

"Of course — I'd expect that. Would it be helpful if I sent you a one-page summary you could share with them before that conversation? That way they have context without you having to explain it from scratch. And I'm also happy to join a call with whoever needs to be in the room — just let me know."

---

## VOICEMAIL SCRIPT
*(Target: 20 seconds — under 30 max)*

"Hi [First Name], this is Aaron Kramer from Two Birds Innovation. I built a digital literacy platform that Ontario credit unions are using to help senior members stay safe online and reduce fraud claims. I'd love to show you — it takes 15 minutes. I'll follow up by email, but feel free to call me back at [YOUR PHONE]. Have a great day."

---

## NOTES FOR USE

- Always pause after the qualifying question. Resist filling silence.
- If they engage with a problem you can solve, slow down and go deeper before jumping to features.
- "15 minutes" is the only ask. Never pitch a 30-minute or 1-hour meeting on a cold call.
- Credit union contacts: CEOs, VP Member Experience, VP Operations, Digital Banking Managers
- Library contacts: CEO/Chief Librarian, Digital Services Manager, Community Programming Lead
- Best call windows: Tuesday–Thursday, 10–11:30 AM and 2–4 PM
