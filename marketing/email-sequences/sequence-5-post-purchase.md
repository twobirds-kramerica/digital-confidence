---

**Quick optimisation tips for month two:**

**Add a QR code at your front desk or service counter.**
Many older adults will explore the platform after being told about it in person. A simple printed card or poster with a QR code and the words "Learn to use your [phone/online banking] safely — scan here" converts in-branch visits into platform users.

**Feature it in your next member communication.**
A single sentence with a personal endorsement from a staff member or branch manager outperforms a generic announcement. Something like: *"We've been getting really positive feedback on this — if you haven't checked it out yet, it's worth 10 minutes."*

**Ask one staff member to go through two modules.**
When your staff have experienced the platform themselves, they talk about it differently. The recommendation lands with more authenticity.
