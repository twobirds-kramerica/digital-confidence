# RBC Future Launch
## Grant Application Template — Digital Confidence Centre

**Program:** RBC Future Launch — Community Investment
**Focus:** Skills development, financial literacy, employment readiness, economic inclusion
**Amount:** Varies — community grants typically $10,000–$25,000; national partnerships larger
**Eligibility:** Registered charities and non-profits; RBC-aligned purpose
**Application:** rbcfoundation.com — or through RBC branch community manager
**Note:** RBC also has a separate "Tech for Good" stream for digital inclusion initiatives

---

## Program Alignment

RBC Future Launch prioritises:
- **Economic resilience** — particularly for underserved populations
- **Digital skills** — increasingly core to RBC's community investment strategy
- **Financial literacy** — RBC's longest-running community focus area
- **Youth** (primary), but seniors are increasingly included under "economic inclusion"

**DCC alignment:** Financial literacy + digital skills for seniors = strong fit for RBC's evolving priorities. Frame around fraud prevention as financial resilience.

## Application Narrative Template

**Project Title:** Building Financial Resilience for Ontario Seniors Through Digital Literacy

**Executive Summary:**
[ORGANIZATION] is seeking $[AMOUNT] from RBC Future Launch to deploy the Digital Confidence Centre — a digital literacy platform that builds financial resilience among Ontario seniors by teaching them to protect themselves from fraud, use online banking safely, and access financial services independently.

**The Financial Impact of Digital Exclusion:**
Ontario seniors lose over $200 million annually to digital fraud targeting financial accounts. Beyond fraud, digital exclusion limits seniors' ability to access their own financial accounts, claim government benefits, and manage their finances independently — creating dependence and vulnerability.

**The Programme:**
The Digital Confidence Centre delivers 15 modules of practical digital skills training, with particular emphasis on:
- Module 2: Recognising and avoiding financial fraud
- Module 6: Online banking safety (including mobile banking apps)
- Module 3: Password and account security
- Module 5: Safe email practices (phishing defence)

All content uses Canadian examples, real Ontario phone numbers, and culturally appropriate framing for adults 70+.

**Partnership with RBC:**
We would welcome the opportunity to incorporate RBC-specific resources into our online banking module, including:
- RBC Mobile app navigation
- RBC Online Banking security features
- RBC Advice Centre digital resources
- RBC fraud reporting contacts

This integration creates direct value for RBC customers who participate in the programme.
