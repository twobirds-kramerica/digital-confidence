## About Starter Company Plus

Starter Company Plus provides training, mentorship, and grants up to $5,000 to help Ontario small businesses and entrepreneurs start or expand. Grants are awarded based on business viability, growth potential, and the applicant's demonstrated commitment to their business.

This application documents Two Birds Innovation's readiness for the next award cycle and the specific growth activities the grant would fund.

**Business name:** Two Birds Innovation

Two Birds Innovation operates in two complementary streams:

**Stream 2 — Aaron Patzalek Consulting**
Product management consulting for technology companies and mission-driven organisations. Services include product strategy, roadmap development, user research facilitation, and team coaching. Primarily remote; clients in Ontario and Canada.

## Section 2 — The Problem and Opportunity

**For DCC:**
Digital exclusion among seniors is growing faster than existing programs can address. Public libraries, community health centres, and senior services organisations across Ontario are actively seeking digital literacy resources they can offer clients — but they lack the capacity to build curriculum themselves and the budget to license commercial platforms.

The Digital Confidence Centre fills this gap: it is free for individuals, requires no setup, and can be delivered with or without a facilitator. The market opportunity is significant — there are over 2,500 public libraries, community health centres, credit unions, and senior services organisations in Ontario, each of which represents a potential partnership.

**For consulting:**
The product management consulting market in Canada is growing. Organisations increasingly recognise that they need experienced product leadership without the cost of a full-time hire. Two Birds Innovation is positioned to serve this market from a base of 15+ years of product management experience, with a specialisation in accessible design and mission-driven technology.

## Section 5 — Competitive Landscape

**Who else serves this need?**

| Competitor | Strengths | Limitations |
|------------|-----------|-------------|
| ISED's Be Cyber Safe | Government backing, bilingual | Generic, not seniors-specific, no modules structure |
| Evergreen Life | Canadian nonprofit, solid curriculum | English only, not iPad-first, not self-paced |
| Commercial platforms (GetSetUp, AARP) | Well-funded, polished | US-focused, subscription-based, not free |
| Local library workshops | Trusted, in-person | Limited capacity, English only, no take-home resource |

**Two Birds Innovation's advantage:**
DCC is the only free, bilingual, WCAG 2.1 AA, iPad-first, self-paced digital literacy platform designed specifically for Canadian seniors. No Canadian competitor combines all five of these characteristics.
