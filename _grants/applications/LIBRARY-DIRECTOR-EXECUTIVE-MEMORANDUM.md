### Executive Summary

Public libraries are the primary civic safety net for older adults navigating an increasingly digital world. Yet frontline library staff bear the brunt of informal tech support: helping seniors recover locked accounts, decipher suspicious emails, or navigate government portals, often without structured pedagogical tools.

**The Opportunity:** Two Birds Innovation has built the **Digital Confidence Centre (DCC)**—a 40+ module, bilingual, zero-barrier digital literacy and scam defense platform specifically designed for older Canadian adults (55+). 

We are offering your library system a **zero-IT-overhead partnership**: a turnkey pilot program for 2027 backed by federal funding via Employment and Social Development Canada’s (ESDC) **New Horizons for Seniors Program (NHSP)**, which provides up to **$50,000** for community-led senior empowerment projects.

---

### Why the Digital Confidence Centre Fits Library Operations

1. **Zero IT Friction & Privacy Preserving:**
   - 100% static client-side web application. Runs on any branch workstation, Chromebook, or iPad.
   - **No logins, no passwords, no software installation, and no personal data collection.** Patrons simply sit down and learn.
2. **Senior-Calibrated Ergonomics:**
   - 19px base font, 56px touch targets, dyslexic font mode, and verified high-contrast surfaces.
   - Tested and refined with real Southwestern Ontario seniors.
3. **High-Stakes, Practical Curriculum (40+ Modules):**
   - Elder financial fraud defense: fake Canada Post delivery texts, bank impersonation calls, CRA refund lures, and payment redirection.
   - Daily digital independence: accessing MyChart/healthcare portals, password management, video calling family, and understanding AI.
4. **Structured Pedagogical Framework:**
   - Each module takes 6–10 minutes, features structured interactive simulations, and ends on a verified practical rule: *"The 3-Second Rule"* and *"The Move"*.

### Federal Funding Architecture (ESDC NHSP)

- **The Grant:** ESDC New Horizons for Seniors Program (Community-Based Stream) awards up to **$50,000 non-repayable project funding** for senior-led, community-based projects up to 52 weeks.
- **The Fit:** Elder abuse prevention and fraud defense are explicit top-tier national funding priorities for ESDC.
- **The Turnkey Model:** Two Birds Innovation prepares the entire grant proposal, provides the Senior Advisory Council structure, and delivers all workshop materials. Grant funds cover library staff honorariums, peer-senior facilitator stipends, printed large-format patron workbooks, and program administration.
