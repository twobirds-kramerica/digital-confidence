## Application Narrative Template

**Project Title:** Digital Security and Financial Literacy for Ontario Seniors: The Digital Confidence Centre

**Need Statement:**
Canada's seniors face a dual threat: rapid digitisation of essential financial services combined with an epidemic of digital fraud. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre reports that financial fraud targeting seniors costs Canadians over $1.6 billion annually, with online/digital scams representing the fastest-growing category.

At the same time, seniors who lack digital confidence are increasingly excluded from basic financial services: online banking, e-transfers, digital receipts, and mobile banking apps are now the primary service channel for most financial institutions.

In [REGION], [DESCRIBE LOCAL CONTEXT — seniors served by partner organization, specific digital access challenges].

**Proposed Solution:**
[ORGANIZATION] will deploy the Digital Confidence Centre — a Canadian-built, WCAG AA accessible digital literacy platform — to serve [X] seniors in [REGION] over [PROJECT DURATION].

The programme prioritises financial security skills:
- **Module 1:** Device safety and scam recognition fundamentals
- **Module 2:** Fraud prevention (CRA scams, phishing, romance fraud)
- **Module 3:** Password and account security
- **Module 5:** Email phishing protection
- **Module 6:** Online banking safety

Additionally, modules covering social connection (Modules 8, 13) and healthcare access (Module 15) address the connected communities and health dimensions of TD's mandate.

**Why This Approach Works:**
- Self-paced: seniors learn without embarrassment at home
- Canadian content: TD-specific examples can be integrated
- Measurable: module completion tracking and quiz pass rates
- Scalable: the same platform reaches rural and urban seniors equally
- No data risk: platform collects no personal information

## Grant Application Tips (TD-Specific)

- **TD prioritises multi-year commitments** — consider applying for a 2–3 year Grow grant
- **Local TD managers can champion your application** — cultivate the relationship first
- **Quantify financial security impact** — use CAFC fraud statistics specific to your region
- **Include a diversity lens** — how does your programme reach seniors with language barriers or disabilities?
- **TD loves measurement** — provide a clear evaluation framework with specific metrics and timelines
