## Assessment Tools

### Tool 1: Pre-Programme Digital Confidence Survey

**When administered:** At enrolment, before any modules are accessed.
**Format:** 5 questions, 5-point Likert scale.
**Estimated completion time:** 3–5 minutes.
**Delivery method:** Online form (embedded in welcome email link) or assisted verbal completion by phone.

---

**Digital Confidence Survey (Pre)**

*Instructions to participant: "For each question below, please choose the number that best describes how you feel right now. 1 means 'Not confident at all' and 5 means 'Very confident.' There are no right or wrong answers."*

1. How confident do you feel using your iPhone or iPad to browse the internet safely?
   1 — Not confident at all / 2 — Slightly confident / 3 — Somewhat confident / 4 — Quite confident / 5 — Very confident

2. How confident do you feel recognising a scam, fraud attempt, or suspicious message online or by phone?
   1 — Not confident at all / 2 — Slightly confident / 3 — Somewhat confident / 4 — Quite confident / 5 — Very confident

3. How confident do you feel using online banking, e-transfers, or digital payment safely?
   1 — Not confident at all / 2 — Slightly confident / 3 — Somewhat confident / 4 — Quite confident / 5 — Very confident

4. How confident do you feel managing your passwords to keep your accounts secure?
   1 — Not confident at all / 2 — Slightly confident / 3 — Somewhat confident / 4 — Quite confident / 5 — Very confident

5. How comfortable do you feel asking for help when you're unsure about something on your device?
   1 — Not comfortable at all / 2 — Slightly comfortable / 3 — Somewhat comfortable / 4 — Quite comfortable / 5 — Very comfortable

**Aggregate pre-programme score:** Sum of all 5 answers (range: 5–25). Record per participant. Average across cohort.

### Tool 3: Post-Programme Assessment (Same 5 Questions as Pre)

**When administered:** Approximately Day 28, after participant has completed at least 2 modules.
**Format:** Same 5 questions as the pre-survey (Tool 1), plus 3 additional questions.
**Estimated completion time:** 8–12 minutes.
**Delivery method:** Online form linked in Day 28 email.

---

**Post-Programme Survey**

*Questions 1–5: Identical to pre-programme survey (for direct comparison).*

**Additional questions:**

6. What was the most useful or interesting thing you learned through Digital Confidence Centre?
   *(Open text)*

7. Would you recommend this programme to a friend, family member, or neighbour?
   Yes, definitely / Probably yes / Probably not / Definitely not

8. Is there any topic you wish the programme had covered, or any feedback you'd like to share with us?
   *(Open text)*

---

**Calculating confidence improvement:**
- Pre-score (sum of Q1–Q5 at enrolment) vs. Post-score (sum of Q1–Q5 post-programme)
- Average improvement per participant = Post-score − Pre-score
- Cohort average improvement = Average of all individual improvements
- Report as: "Participants' average confidence score increased by [X] points (from [X]/25 to [X]/25)"

**Reporting threshold:** A meaningful improvement is generally considered to be +1.5 or more on the 5-point scale across questions, or +7 or more on the 25-point aggregate.

**Data source:** GitHub Pages / platform usage data; quiz submission records.

## Data Analysis Guide

### How to Calculate Confidence Improvement %

1. For each participant who completed both pre and post surveys, record their 5 individual scores and total (out of 25) at each point.
2. Calculate the difference: Post-total − Pre-total = Individual improvement score.
3. Average all individual improvement scores across the cohort = Cohort average improvement.
4. To express as a percentage: (Cohort average improvement / 25) × 100 = % improvement of maximum possible score.

**Example:**
- 20 participants completed pre and post surveys
- Average pre-score: 11.5 / 25
- Average post-score: 16.2 / 25
- Average improvement: +4.7 points
- % of maximum: (4.7 / 25) × 100 = 18.8% improvement
- Simpler reporting: "Participants' average confidence score increased by 4.7 points out of 25 (a 41% improvement from their baseline score)"

### How to Report Quiz Pass Rates

1. Pull total quiz submissions per module from platform data.
2. Count submissions with score ≥ 70%.
3. Pass rate = (Passing submissions / Total submissions) × 100.
4. Report per module and as a programme average.

### How to Handle Incomplete Data

- Participants who did not complete the post-survey should be excluded from confidence improvement calculations (not counted as "no improvement").
- Note the number excluded and the reason (if known: withdrew, inactive, lost contact) in the report.
- Never impute or estimate missing scores.
