# Letter of Support Template — Partner Organisations

**Purpose:** Formal letter of support from a registered charity or non-profit partner, submitted with DCC grant applications.
**Owner:** Two Birds Innovation
**Last Updated:** March 2026

---

## Letterhead Instructions

The letter must be printed on your partner organisation's official letterhead, which typically includes:

- Organisation logo (top centre or top left)
- Organisation name in full
- Mailing address, city, province, postal code
- Phone number and website
- CRA charity registration number (for registered charities)

If your partner organisation does not have a standard letterhead template, they should create one in a word processor before drafting this letter. The letter will look unprofessional without letterhead and some funders will reject it.

The letter should be:
- Signed in ink by the Executive Director, President, or equivalent senior leader
- Dated within 3 months of the application submission date
- Addressed to the specific funder (not "To Whom It May Concern" where avoidable)

## General Notes for All Variants

**Timing:** Request the partner letter at least 3 weeks before the grant deadline. Executive Directors are busy; give them enough time to review, revise, and sign.

**Customisation:** Share a draft with the partner organisation and invite them to edit freely. The letter will be more compelling in their authentic voice. Do not require them to use the exact language above.

**Specificity:** The more specific the letter is about the partner organisation's community, their direct experience of the problem, and their concrete commitment to the project, the more persuasive it will be. Generic letters from well-known organisations are less effective than specific, genuine letters from smaller local ones.

**Avoid duplication:** The letter should complement the application narrative — not repeat it verbatim. The partner should speak to their own experience and perspective, not simply paraphrase what is already in the project description.

**CRA number:** Make sure the organisation's CRA charity registration number appears on their letterhead or in the body of the letter. Some funders require it; it's always reassuring to include it.
