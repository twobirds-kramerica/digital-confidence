## How to Use This Template

1. Choose the scenario closest to your target ask ($10k, $25k, or $75k).
2. Replace bracketed values with your actual costs and estimates.
3. Check funder guidelines for overhead caps, eligible expenses, and matching fund requirements.
4. Share the completed budget with your charity partner's financial administrator for sign-off before submission.

## Budget Categories Overview

**Notes on personnel:**

### Category C — Indirect / Overhead

Overhead covers the partner organisation's administrative costs for managing the grant (accounting, insurance, HR, facilities).

**Standard cap:** 15% of direct costs (cash only, excluding in-kind).

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Partner organisation administrative overhead | 15% × (Personnel Cash + Direct Costs Cash) | $[X] |
| **Overhead Subtotal** | | **$[X]** |

**Funder-specific overhead limits:**
- Ontario Trillium Foundation: typically accepts up to 15%
- New Horizons for Seniors: no strict cap stated, but keep below 20% for competitiveness
- Ontario Seniors Community Grant: no explicit cap, but reviewers scrutinise high overhead
- Google.org: typically accepts 12–15% for Canadian applications
- Corporate funders (RBC, TD, Bell, Telus): prefer overhead below 15%; some specify 10%

## Matching Funds: How to Calculate

Some funders require or prefer "matching" contributions — meaning the project must demonstrate funding or in-kind contributions from sources other than the grant.

**Example: 1:1 match requirement on a $25,000 ask**
- Funder provides: $25,000
- Match required: $25,000
- Your match can come from: in-kind (Two Birds platform value), partner org in-kind (staff time, facilities), other grants, or cash contributions from the organisation

**Calculating in-kind as match:**
Most funders will accept documented in-kind contributions as match. Valuation must be reasonable and verifiable:
- Staff time: use actual wage rates, not inflated consultant rates
- Platform/technology: use amortised development cost or equivalent commercial licence value
- Facilities: use published rental rates for equivalent space

**Important:** Some funders specify that matching funds must be "new money" raised specifically for this project, not previously committed operating budgets. Read the guidelines carefully.

## Budget Checklist (Before Submitting)

- [ ] All line items have a clear justification (what it pays for, why it's necessary)
- [ ] Personnel rates are consistent with partner org's pay scales
- [ ] Overhead is at or below the funder's stated cap
- [ ] In-kind contributions are separately listed and documented
- [ ] Concurrent grant applications are disclosed (if applicable)
- [ ] Budget has been reviewed by partner org's financial administrator
- [ ] Total adds up correctly (double-check the arithmetic)
- [ ] Budget narrative / notes accompany the table (most funders require this)
- [ ] Canadian dollars specified (CAD) throughout
