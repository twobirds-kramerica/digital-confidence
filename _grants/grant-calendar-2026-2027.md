## Q2 2026 (April–June)

## Notes on Two Birds Innovation's Grant Eligibility

Two Birds Innovation is currently a for-profit entity. This limits direct eligibility for many grants. Options:

1. **Partner with a registered charity** — The charity applies; Two Birds provides services under contract. Most common approach.
2. **Incorporate a non-profit arm** — Longer-term option; consult a lawyer.
3. **Apply to for-profit eligible programs** — Some NHSP streams, some corporate community grants, some ISED programs allow for-profits.

**Recommended near-term strategy:** Identify one to two registered charity partners (library, senior centre, YMCA) who can apply with DCC as the programme they're delivering. Two Birds provides services under a contract funded by the grant.
