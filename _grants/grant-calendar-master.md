## Grant Writing Workflow

### Timeline by Application Type

**Small grant (under $10,000) — 3-week timeline**

| Week | Task |
|------|------|
| Week 1 | Confirm eligibility; gather partner letter; adapt project description from master templates |
| Week 2 | Draft full application; internal review; partner review |
| Week 3 | Revise; final proofread; submit with 3+ days before deadline |

**Medium grant ($10,000–$75,000) — 6-week timeline**

| Week | Task |
|------|------|
| Week 1 | Confirm eligibility and partner capacity; review funder guidelines |
| Week 2 | Draft project description narrative; adapt budget from master template |
| Week 3 | Draft full application; share with partner org for review |
| Week 4 | Incorporate partner feedback; finalise evaluation plan; draft sustainability section |
| Week 5 | Full application review; proofread; gather supporting documents |
| Week 6 | Final revisions; partner sign-off; submit 5+ days before deadline |

**Large grant (over $75,000) — 10–12 week timeline**

| Week | Task |
|------|------|
| Weeks 1–2 | Confirm eligibility; secure charity partner commitment; review all guidelines |
| Weeks 3–4 | Draft project narrative (Section 5); stakeholder review |
| Weeks 5–6 | Draft budget, evaluation plan, and sustainability section |
| Weeks 7–8 | Full draft circulated to partner org and any advisors; incorporate feedback |
| Weeks 9–10 | Revise full application; legal/financial review with partner org |
| Weeks 11–12 | Final proofread; assemble supporting documents; partner signatures; submit |

## Budget Management Across Multiple Concurrent Grants

### Key Rules

1. **Never double-count costs.** If Aaron's time is charged to Grant A, it cannot also be charged to Grant B for the same hours. Keep a time tracking log.

2. **Track in-kind contributions separately.** In-kind (Two Birds' platform investment, partner facilities) can typically be claimed across multiple applications as context/leverage — but not as "matching funds" from two funders simultaneously.

3. **Grant funds cannot cross-subsidise each other.** Each grant must fund discrete, documented activities. Keep separate folders of receipts and timesheets per grant.

4. **Report revenue to your charity partner.** All grant funds will flow through the partner organisation's accounts. Maintain clear written agreements about what Two Birds invoices for services delivered.

5. **Maintain a grant revenue log.**

### Managing Funder Conflicts

Some funders (particularly federal and provincial) prohibit receiving both grants for the same project costs. If applying for NHSP and OSCG simultaneously:

- Assign different budget line items to each grant (e.g., NHSP covers participant outreach costs; OSCG covers programme coordination)
- Disclose concurrent applications in both applications (most funders require this)
- Confirm with your charity partner's financial administrator before submitting concurrent applications
