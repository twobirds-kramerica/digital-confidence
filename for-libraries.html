A funding option worth knowing about

Many Canadian institutions can apply for the federal New Horizons for Seniors Program (NHSP), run by Employment and Social Development Canada, to cover this fee as part of a seniors-focused community project. Mention it when you get in touch below and we can talk through whether it fits your organisation.
