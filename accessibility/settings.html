Accessibility Settings

Adjust how the Digital Confidence Centre looks and works for you. Your settings are saved automatically.

Text Size

Choose a text size that is comfortable for reading.

Colour & Contrast

Dark Mode

Dark background with light text — easier on the eyes in low light.

High Contrast

Stronger colour contrast for text and buttons — helps if you have low vision.

Reading Aids

Reading Guide

A horizontal highlight bar follows your cursor to help you track which line you're reading.

Increased Text Spacing

More space between letters, words, and lines — helps with dyslexia and reading fatigue.

Reduce Animations

Turns off page transitions and moving elements — helps if movement is distracting or causes discomfort.

Reset Settings

Return all display settings to their defaults.

Settings are saved in your browser and never sent to us.
