How do I update my apps?

Keeping your apps updated is one of the best ways to protect your device. Here is how to do it on an iPhone or iPad — it takes about 2 minutes:

Update apps on iPhone or iPad

  1. Make sure you are connected to Wi-Fi (the fan symbol at the top of your screen)
  2. Open the App Store — the blue icon with a white letter A on your home screen
  3. Tap your profile photo in the top right corner of the screen
  4. Scroll down a little — you will see a list of apps with a blue Update button next to each
  5. Tap Update All to update everything at once. Wait for it to finish — a loading circle will spin next to each app.

Turn on automatic updates — so it happens by itself

  1. Open Settings (the grey icon that looks like gears)
  2. Tap App Store
  3. Under Automatic Downloads, tap the green switch next to App Updates to turn it on

Once this is on, your phone will update apps overnight while charging — no action needed from you.

Update your phone's software too

Apps are not the only thing that needs updating. Your iPhone or iPad's operating system (iOS) also gets important security updates. Go to Settings → General → Software Update to check for them.
