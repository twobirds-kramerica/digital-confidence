How do I know if a phone call is a scam?

Most scam calls share the same tricks. Here are the five warning signs to listen for:

  • They pressure you to act right now. A real organization will always let you call back.
  • They ask for payment in gift cards, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency. No legitimate company or government agency does this.
  • They threaten you with arrest, deportation, or account suspension. This is scare tactics — not how real agencies work.
  • They claim to be the CRA, Service Canada, your bank, Apple, or Microsoft calling about a problem.
  • They tell you to keep the call a secret. Real organizations never ask you to do this.
The 3-Second Rule

If a call makes you feel scared or rushed — pause 3 seconds. Then hang up. You can always call back. A real organization will understand.

What to do if you suspect a scam call

  • Hang up. You do not owe the caller anything.
  • Do not call back the number that called you — scammers use real-looking numbers.
  • If worried about your bank account, call the number on the back of your bank card.
  • Report scam calls to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: 1-888-495-8501
