What is a strong password?

A strong password makes it very hard for criminals to guess or break into your accounts. Here is what makes a password strong:

  • At least 12 characters long (longer is better)
  • A mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols (like ! or #)
  • Different for every account (your email password is not the same as your bank password)
  • Does not include your name, birthday, or pet's name

The easy way: use a passphrase

A passphrase is 3 or 4 random words joined together. It is easy to remember but very hard to crack.

Example: PurpleTulip!RainyBoat22

This is 24 characters long, uses a symbol and numbers, and is easy to write down and remember.

Where to keep your passwords

  • Write them in a small notebook kept in a safe place at home — not labelled "passwords"
  • Use the Passwords app on iPhone/iPad (Settings → Passwords) — it is free and secure
  • Never save passwords in a text message, email, or note you can see on screen
