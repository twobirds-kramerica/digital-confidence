How do I video call my grandchildren?

Video calling is free and easier than you might think. Here are the two most common ways:

Option 1: FaceTime (iPhone or iPad only)

FaceTime is already on your iPhone or iPad — you do not need to download anything.

  1. Open the green FaceTime app (it looks like a video camera)
  2. Tap the + button in the top right corner
  3. Type your grandchild's name or phone number
  4. Tap the green Video button to start calling

Option 2: WhatsApp (works on any phone)

WhatsApp works if you and your grandchild have different types of phones (one iPhone, one Android). It is free over Wi-Fi.

  1. Open WhatsApp and find your grandchild's name in your contacts
  2. Tap their name to open a conversation
  3. Tap the video camera icon in the top right corner
  4. The call will ring on their phone — wait for them to answer
Make sure you are on Wi-Fi

Video calls use more data than regular calls. To avoid charges, look for the Wi-Fi symbol (the fan shape) at the top of your screen before calling. If you see it, you are on Wi-Fi — free to call!
