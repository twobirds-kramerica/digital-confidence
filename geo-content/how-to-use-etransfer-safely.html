How do I use e-transfer safely?

Interac e-Transfer is a safe and convenient way to send money in Canada — as long as you follow a few rules. Here is how to use it securely:

When sending an e-transfer

  • Only send to people you know personally — not to strangers, sellers on Marketplace, or anyone you met online
  • Double-check the recipient's email address or phone number before confirming. One wrong letter sends real money to the wrong person.
  • Choose a security question whose answer is not publicly available (not your pet's name, street name, or birthdate)
  • Share the security question answer via a separate method — call the person, do not text or email the answer alongside the transfer

Set up Autodeposit — the safest option

Autodeposit sends incoming e-transfers straight to your bank account without a security question. This is actually safer because there is no answer for a scammer to steal.

Set it up in your bank's app under Interac e-Transfer settings, or ask your bank to help you turn it on.

Never send an e-transfer to cancel or refund one

A common scam: someone sends you a fake e-transfer then contacts you saying it was sent by mistake or is too large, and asks you to send part of it back. The original transfer was fake — but the one you send back is real. Your bank cannot undo it.
