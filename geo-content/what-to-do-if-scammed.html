What should I do if I think I've been scammed?

⚠️ You are not in trouble and you are not alone.
Scammers are professional criminals. Thousands of Canadians are targeted every month. Reporting is the right thing to do.

If you have given money or personal information to a scammer, here are the four steps to take right now:

  1. Stop all contact with the scammer. Do not reply to their messages, take their calls, or send any more money — even if they try to convince you it will get your money back.
  2. Call your bank immediately. Use the number on the back of your bank card or a number you find on your bank's official website. Tell them what happened. They may be able to stop or reverse the transaction.
  3. Report to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: call 1-888-495-8501 or visit antifraudcentre.ca. They track scams across Canada and help shut down criminal operations.
  4. Report to local police. Call the non-emergency line for your city or visit your local station. Ask for a file number — you will need it for insurance and to report identity theft.
Watch out for the follow-up scam

After being scammed, you may be contacted by someone claiming to be from the government or a recovery service who can get your money back for a fee. This is also a scam. No legitimate organization charges money to recover scam losses.
