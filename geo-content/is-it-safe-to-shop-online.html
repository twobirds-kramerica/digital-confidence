Is it safe to shop online?

Yes — online shopping is safe when you use trusted websites and pay with a credit card. Here is what to look for:

Trusted Canadian shopping sites

  • Amazon.ca — wide selection, easy returns, reliable
  • Walmart.ca — groceries, household items, same prices as the store
  • Indigo.ca — books, gifts, and home items
  • CanadianTire.ca — hardware, seasonal items, outdoor gear

Before you enter your payment details

  • Look for the padlock symbol in the address bar and "https://" at the start of the web address — this means the site is secure
  • Pay with a credit card, not a debit card. Credit cards have fraud protection — if something goes wrong, your bank can dispute the charge
  • Never pay by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency — these cannot be reversed
If a price seems too good to be true…

Search the store's name plus the word "scam" on Google. If many people have complained about it, you will see results quickly. A real sale of 20–30% off is believable. An 80% off sale on a designer item from a site you have never heard of is almost always a fake.
