How do I set up online banking safely?

Online banking is safe when you follow a few simple rules. Here are the five steps to get started securely:

  1. Set up with your bank directly. Call your bank or go in person to get your online banking login. Do not set it up from a link in an email or text message.
  2. Download only the official bank app. Go to the App Store and search for your bank's name. Make sure it says "Royal Bank of Canada" or your bank's full official name — not a look-alike app.
  3. Create a strong password. Use at least 12 characters. Do not use your name, birthdate, or the same password you use for other accounts. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe at home — not on your phone.
  4. Turn on notifications. In your bank's app, turn on push notifications or email alerts for every transaction. This way you will know immediately if anything unusual happens.
  5. Only bank on your home Wi-Fi or mobile data. Never use public Wi-Fi (at a cafe, library, or hotel) for banking. If you are not at home, use your phone's mobile data instead.
Your bank will never ask for your full password

If anyone — even someone who says they are from your bank — asks for your online banking password or PIN, do not give it. Call your bank at the number on the back of your card immediately.
