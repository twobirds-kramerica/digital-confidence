## EMAIL 3 — Day 8: Personal Note from Aaron

**Subject:** A personal note — from the person who built this

**Preview text:** This one is just from me.

---

Hello,

My name is Aaron, and I built the Digital Confidence Centre.

I'm writing to you directly — not as an organisation, just as a person who spent a lot of time trying to make something genuinely useful.

I started this project because I was watching people I care about feel increasingly left behind by technology. Not because they weren't capable — they absolutely are — but because nobody had taken the time to explain things in a way that felt patient, honest, and built for them.

Every module on this site, every word of every lesson, was written with that in mind. No jargon. No condescension. No rush.

If you tried the site and something didn't work for you — the language was too technical, a step was unclear, something on your iPad behaved differently than what we described — I want to know. Not to defend it, but to fix it. You can reply to this email and I'll read it myself.

And if life simply got busy, that's completely fine. The site will still be here in a month, in six months, whenever you feel ready to come back.

I just wanted you to know that someone built this with care, and that you matter to this project.

Take care of yourself.

Aaron
