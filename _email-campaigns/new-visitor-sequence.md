## EMAIL 3 — Day 7: Scam Awareness Tip

**Subject:** "The CRA called me — and I nearly fell for it"

**Preview text:** A real scam, and the three words that stop it cold.

---

Hello,

Every week, thousands of Canadians receive calls from someone pretending to be the Canada Revenue Agency. The calls are alarming — the caller says you owe back taxes, that police are on their way, that you need to pay immediately or face arrest.

Many people — including sharp, intelligent adults — have sent money because of these calls. It's not a failure of intelligence. It's a failure of information.

Here are three things to know right now:

1. **The real CRA does not call to threaten arrest.** If someone says police are coming, hang up.
2. **No government agency in Canada will ever ask for gift cards.** If they ask you to buy iTunes cards or Google Play cards to pay a debt — it's a scam, always.
3. **You are always allowed to hang up.** You don't owe a scammer politeness.

Module 2 at Digital Confidence Centre covers this in detail — including what to do if you've already responded to a scam call.

**[Explore Module 2: The Security Shield →](https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca/module-2.html)**

Stay safe out there,
