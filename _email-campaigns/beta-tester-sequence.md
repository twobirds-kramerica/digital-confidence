# Beta Tester Email Sequence
**Trigger:** Beta application approved / Welcome email sent manually or via Formspree automation
**Sequence Length:** 4 emails over 21 days
**Audience:** Accepted beta testers

---

## EMAIL 1 — Immediate: Welcome to the Beta Programme

**Subject:** You're in — welcome to the Digital Confidence Centre beta team

**Preview text:** We're so glad to have you. Here's what to expect.

---

Hello,

Your application has been reviewed and we're delighted to welcome you to the Digital Confidence Centre beta testing programme.

This is a small, select group of Ontarians helping us make this resource as good as it can be for older adults across the province. Your contribution — your honest feedback, your time, your patience with anything that isn't quite working yet — is genuinely valuable to us.

**Here's what happens next:**

Over the coming weeks, we'll invite you to try new modules and features before they go live. After each session, we'll ask you to share your thoughts through a simple feedback form on the site. There are no right or wrong answers — we want your real experience, not what you think we want to hear.

**To get started:**
- Visit the beta welcome page: **[digitalconfidencecentre.ca/beta-welcome.html](https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca/beta-welcome.html)**
- Explore the site at your own pace
- Note anything that confuses you, excites you, or could be clearer

Your first formal check-in will arrive by email in one week.

Thank you again for being part of this. We're building something together.

Aaron
Digital Confidence Centre

## EMAIL 2 — Day 7: Week 1 Check-In

**Subject:** One week in — how's it going?

**Preview text:** Honest answer only, please. That's what helps us.

---

Hello,

You've had a week to explore the Digital Confidence Centre. We'd love to hear how it's going.

A few things we're curious about:

**What felt easy?** Which parts of the site felt natural and comfortable to navigate?

**What felt confusing?** Was there anything that made you pause, backtrack, or feel unsure? Even small things matter — a button that wasn't obvious, wording that wasn't clear, a step that seemed to be missing.

**What would you add?** Is there a topic or skill you expected to find that isn't there yet?

You can share your thoughts through the feedback form on any module page, or simply reply to this email. Both routes reach us directly.

**[Leave Feedback on the Site →](https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca)**

There are no grades here. No right answers. Everything you share, even "I found this confusing and I'm not sure why," is exactly what we need.

Thank you for your time and your honesty.

Aaron
Digital Confidence Centre

## EMAIL 3 — Day 14: Please Share Your Feedback

**Subject:** Two weeks in — your feedback is making a real difference

**Preview text:** Could you take 5 minutes today? It matters more than you know.

---

Hello,

You're two weeks into the beta programme, and we want to share something with you.

The feedback we've received so far has already led to changes on the site. Words that were confusing have been rewritten. A button that was hard to find has been moved. A step in one of the exercises has been simplified. None of that would have happened without testers like you.

If you haven't had a chance to leave detailed feedback yet, now is the perfect time. We're particularly interested in:

- Whether the modules feel the right length (too long? too short?)
- Whether the language feels appropriate — not too simple, not too technical
- Whether there are topics you wish were covered that aren't
- How the site feels on an iPad specifically — is everything easy to read and tap?

**[Share Your Feedback →](https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca)**

Or simply reply to this email with your thoughts. That works just as well.

Your honest input — even if it's critical — is the most helpful thing you can offer right now.

Thank you for sticking with this.

Aaron
Digital Confidence Centre
