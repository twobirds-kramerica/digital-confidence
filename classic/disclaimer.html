It is not legal advice, financial advice, cybersecurity advice, or professional advice of any kind, and it is not a substitute for the judgment of a qualified professional who knows your situation. If you think you are being scammed, act right away

If you think you are being scammed, or that money or personal information is at risk, act on it right away. Do not rely only on this website.

  • Contact your bank or financial institution directly using the phone number on the back of your card or on your official statement, not a number someone gave you.
  • In an emergency, call 9-1-1.
  • To report fraud in Canada, contact the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at 1-888-495-8501 or your local police.
  • For legal, financial, or security decisions, speak with a licensed professional.
