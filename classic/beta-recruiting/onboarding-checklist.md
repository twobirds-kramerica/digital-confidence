## Part 2: Day 2 Phone Check-In Script

**Purpose:** Confirm device access, welcome the participant personally, and remove any barriers to getting started.

**Duration:** 10–15 minutes
**Tone:** Warm, unhurried, non-technical

---

### Opening (1–2 minutes)

> "Hi, is this [First Name]? This is Aaron calling from Digital Confidence Centre — is now a good time for a quick chat?"

*[If not a good time: "No problem at all — when would be a good time for me to call back? I'll put it in my calendar."]*

> "Wonderful. I just wanted to call personally to welcome you to the programme and make sure you have everything you need to get started. I'm really glad you're taking part."

---

### Confirm Device Access (2–3 minutes)

> "First, can I just confirm what kind of device you'll be using? Is it an iPhone, or an iPad?"

*Log device type in tracker.*

> "Perfect. And do you have internet access at home — either Wi-Fi or mobile data?"

*If yes: great, move on.*
*If uncertain: "That's okay — let's figure that out together. When you're at home, do you have a Wi-Fi router? Or do you use data through your phone plan?"*

*If they don't have reliable internet:*
> "Don't worry — the programme works on a library or senior centre Wi-Fi as well. We can make this work for you."

---

### Walk Through Getting Started (3–4 minutes)

> "The programme lives on our website — you don't need to download any app. I'm going to give you the address, and if you'd like, we can try opening it together right now while we're on the phone."

> "The address is: [WEBSITE URL]. That's [spell it out slowly]."

> "Can you see the website? ... Great. You'll see a list of lessons across the top. Each one takes about 15 to 20 minutes. You can stop and come back any time — it saves your spot."

> "I'd suggest starting with Module 1 or 2 — they're both a great introduction."

---

### Explain the Module Structure (1–2 minutes)

> "Each module has a few short pages to read, maybe a short video or two, and a simple quiz at the end — just to help the ideas stick. There are no wrong answers that matter; it's just for your own learning."

> "After you finish a module, you'll see a short feedback form. That's where your input comes in — it's just a few questions about what was clear, what was confusing, and how it made you feel. Completely honest answers are the most helpful."

---

### Answer Questions

> "Before I let you go — do you have any questions at all? About the website, the lessons, or anything else?"

*Listen fully. Do not rush. Common questions and answers are in Part 3 below.*

---

### Close

> "You're all set. I'll send you a quick email with our website address and my contact information, just so you have it handy. Any time you have a question — even a small one — you can text or email me at [YOUR PHONE] or [YOUR EMAIL]. I'm here to help."

> "Thank you again, [First Name]. I think you're going to enjoy this. Talk to you soon!"

## Part 3: Staff and Volunteer Briefing Guide

*For library staff, senior centre coordinators, volunteers, and any organisation deploying or referring participants to Digital Confidence Centre.*

---

### How to Introduce the Programme to Participants

**Keep it simple and warm. Suggested language:**

> "We've partnered with a local organisation called Two Birds Innovation to offer a free online learning programme for seniors. It's called Digital Confidence Centre, and it teaches you how to use your iPhone or iPad more safely and confidently — at your own pace, from home.
>
> It's completely free, and there's no pressure. You'd be one of the first people to try it before it launches to the public, which means your feedback will actually shape the final product.
>
> Would you like to hear more about it?"

**Helpful framing for hesitant seniors:**
- "You don't need to be good with technology — that's exactly who this is for."
- "It's not a test. There are no right or wrong answers."
- "You can stop at any time. There's no commitment."
- "Aaron, who runs the programme, is very approachable — you can call him directly if anything isn't working."

---

### Common Questions and Answers

**"Do I need to download an app?"**
> No. It's a website. You just open it on your phone or tablet like any other website.

**"Is it really free? What's the catch?"**
> There is no catch. It's a community service project. There's no cost to participate, now or ever.

**"What if I'm not good with technology?"**
> That's exactly who the programme is designed for. It assumes no prior knowledge and explains everything in plain language.

**"What will they do with my feedback?"**
> Aaron uses the feedback to improve the programme before it launches publicly. No personal information is shared or published without permission.

**"Do I have to complete every lesson?"**
> No. You're encouraged to complete 2–3 modules over the 4 weeks, but there's no obligation to do every lesson.

**"What if I can't figure out how to get on the website?"**
> Call or email Aaron directly at [YOUR PHONE] / [YOUR EMAIL]. He will personally help you get started.

**"Is my information private?"**
> Yes. Feedback is used only for programme development. Personal details are not shared publicly. Testimonials are only used with the participant's written approval.

---

### When to Escalate to Two Birds Innovation

Contact Aaron at [YOUR EMAIL] or [YOUR PHONE] if:

- A participant reports a technical problem accessing the website or modules
- A participant is confused about the programme structure and needs a personal walkthrough
- A participant has concerns about privacy or data use
- A participant wants to withdraw from the beta
- You receive feedback suggesting the programme content is confusing, offensive, or inaccessible
- A participant becomes distressed or frustrated during a session at your facility

**What to say while waiting for Aaron to respond:**
> "I'm going to pass your question along to the programme director, who will be in touch with you directly. In the meantime, there's no pressure to do anything — just take your time."

---

### What You Do NOT Need to Do

- You do not need to teach the programme content yourself
- You do not need to troubleshoot the website
- You do not need to collect or store feedback forms
- You do not need to follow up with participants on Aaron's behalf unless you choose to

Your role is simply to make the introduction and help participants feel comfortable reaching out to Aaron. Everything else is handled by Two Birds Innovation.
