Your Most Important Skill: The Escape Hatch

Before anything else, know this: you can always close something that frightens you. This is your most powerful tool.

The Escape Hatch: Your #1 Safety Tool

Every device has a way to immediately close anything that is causing you worry:

  • iPad / iPhone: Press the Home button (round button at the bottom) or swipe up from the bottom edge to return to your Home screen safely.
  • Windows PC: Press the Windows key on your keyboard (looks like a flag icon) to go back to your Start menu.
  • Mac: Press Command + H to hide the current app, or click the red circle (●) at the top left of any window to close it.

Use it whenever you feel uncertain. You cannot harm your device by closing something.

Spotting Fake Alerts (and Why First Credit Union Will Never Do This)

One of the most common tricks used against seniors is the fake alert. Here is what it looks like and how to handle it.

What a Fake Alert Looks Like

You are browsing online when suddenly the screen fills with a flashing message: "CRITICAL SECURITY ALERT! Your device is infected! Call 1-888-555-9876 IMMEDIATELY or your data will be lost!"

Often there is a loud alarm sound. The screen may seem frozen. It looks terrifying.

This is a scam. It is a website trick, not a real alert from your device.

What First Credit Union Will NEVER Do
  • We will never ask you to call a number that appeared in a pop-up
  • We will never ask for your full password over the phone or by email
  • We will never ask you to send gift cards as payment
  • We will never send you an alarming pop-up telling you to call us
  • If you receive something claiming to be from us, call our official number on the back of your credit union card to verify

The 3-Second Rule

This simple habit will protect you from the vast majority of online threats.

Stop → Breathe → Verify

Any time something online feels urgent, scary, or too good to be true: use the 3-Second Rule.

✋ Stop Take your fingers off the screen. Do not tap, click, or call.
🫁 Breathe One slow breath. Urgency is the scammer's main weapon.
🔍 Verify Contact the real organisation using a number you already know, not one from the message.

What to Do When Your Device Freezes

Frozen screens are common and almost never serious. Here is the correct order of steps:

The "Unfreeze Ladder": Try These in Order

  1. Wait 30 seconds. Some apps just need a moment to catch up.
  2. Press Home / Swipe Up to escape to your home screen.
  3. Force-quit the frozen app: Double-click Home or swipe up slowly, find the app, swipe it off the screen.
  4. Restart your device: Hold the side button → "Slide to Power Off" → wait 30 seconds → power back on.
  5. Call First Credit Union or a trusted family member if the issue persists after a restart.
