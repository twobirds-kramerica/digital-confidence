## GCOS SECTION B — Project Description

### B1. What is your project? (1,000 words max)

Two Birds Innovation will deliver **Digital Confidence: Senior Digital Literacy for Elgin and Middlesex**, a structured 12-month community digital literacy programme for adults aged 55 and older across St. Thomas, London (east end), Aylmer, and Dutton-Dunwich. The programme uses the Digital Confidence Centre (DCC), a free, Canadian-built online learning platform developed by Two Birds Innovation and available at no cost to all participants and partner organisations.

DCC provides 30 self-paced adult learning modules covering foundational digital skills (online safety, device basics, video calling, online banking), 27 plain-language guides to Canadian federal and provincial benefits (CPP, OAS, GIS, CDCP, CRA MyAccount, Service Canada), and an AI literacy track aligned with Employment and Social Development Canada's AI for All strategy (June 2026). All content is Canadian-specific, WCAG 2.1 AA accessible, and requires no login, account creation, or personal data submission.

**Delivery model:** Each workshop is 90 minutes, facilitated by a trained staff member or volunteer coordinator at a partner venue (library branch, community centre, or senior centre). Groups of 8–12 participants work through a guided DCC module together, supported by a printed reference card. At the end of each session, participants complete DCC's built-in five-question knowledge check; facilitators download a session CSV report showing before/after scores and knowledge gains.

**Target population:** Adults aged 55 and older in Elgin and Middlesex County. Priority outreach to: low-income seniors on fixed incomes, recent immigrants aged 55+, rural residents with limited access to in-person digital support, and seniors newly transitioning to online government services (CRA, Service Canada, OHIP+). Partner venues will actively recruit from their existing senior programming registries.

**Workshop topics (Year 1):**
1. Online safety and scam recognition (2 sessions per quarter)
2. Canadian government benefits — finding what you're owed (2 sessions per quarter)
3. Video calling and staying connected (2 sessions per quarter)
4. Online banking safely (1 session per quarter)
5. Understanding AI: what it is, when to trust it, what to ignore (1 session per quarter)

Total workshops: 32 (8 per quarter × 4 quarters)
Total participants (estimated): 256–320 (8–10 per session)

**Partner venues confirmed (letters of support attached / to be requested):**
- St. Thomas Public Library (main branch + Elgin County Library branches)
- Catfish Creek Conservation Authority Seniors Programming (Aylmer)
- Dutton Dunwich Township Community Centre

**Facilitator model:** Aaron Patzalek (Two Birds Innovation founder) delivers all Year 1 workshops and trains one volunteer co-facilitator per venue by Q2. All facilitators receive a printed facilitator guide with session scripts, common questions, and accessibility accommodations.

**Post-session continuity:** Every participant receives a two-page take-home reference card (printed from DCC print-ready PDFs). They are shown how to access DCC from any device at home or at the library — no account required. Participants who wish to continue independently can work through any of the 30 adult modules at their own pace.

### B2. Who will benefit and how many people? 

**Primary beneficiaries:** Adults aged 55 and older, specifically:
- Seniors with limited prior digital experience (first device, newly smartphone-equipped)
- Seniors navigating online government services for the first time (CRA MyAccount, Service Canada)
- Low-income seniors who need to access benefits online but face a digital access barrier
- Rural seniors in Elgin County with limited access to in-person technical support

**Estimated reach (Year 1):**
- Direct programme participants: 256–320 adults
- Indirect reach (family members shown DCC by participants): estimated 80–100

**Geographic area served:** St. Thomas, Aylmer, Rodney, Dutton, and select London (east end) venues — all within a 60 km service radius of St. Thomas.

---

### B3. What outcomes will you achieve?

1. **Increased digital knowledge:** Measurable improvement in online safety, government benefits navigation, and AI literacy as evidenced by DCC's pre/post knowledge assessments.
2. **Increased digital confidence:** Participants self-report higher confidence using the internet, online banking, and government portals at session end.
3. **Reduced social isolation:** Participants connect with peers in structured group learning; facilitators track ongoing return attendance.
4. **Increased benefit uptake awareness:** Participants learn which federal/provincial benefits they may be eligible for and how to access them online.
5. **Sustained independent access:** Participants can access DCC without a facilitator at any time — removing dependency on the programme for continued learning.

## GCOS SECTION C — Outcome Measurement

Outcomes will be measured using DCC's built-in knowledge assessment system. Each module includes a matched five-question pre- and post-session knowledge check. At the end of each workshop, the facilitator downloads a session CSV report from DCC's Admin Export Panel (twobirds-kramerica.github.io/digital-confidence/admin.html) showing: module completed, session date, before score, after score, knowledge gain delta, confidence before and after, and time on module. No identifiable participant data is collected at any point.

**Quarterly reporting indicators:**
1. Total participants served (cumulative)
2. Average knowledge gain per session (before vs. after score delta)
3. Average confidence gain per session
4. Percentage of participants showing positive knowledge delta
5. Number of sessions delivered vs. planned
6. Number of distinct venues active

**Year-end report to ESDC will include:**
- Aggregate outcome data from all 32 workshops
- Participant feedback summary (optional supplementary feedback card)
- Facilitator reflections and content improvement notes
- Continuation plan for Year 2

## GCOS SECTION E — NHSP Priority Alignment

This project directly addresses the NHSP priority of **social participation and inclusion of seniors** by:

1. **Reducing digital isolation** — Seniors who cannot safely navigate online government portals, banking, or communication tools face growing barriers to independence. Our programme removes these barriers with Canadian-specific, accessible content.

2. **Improving access to government benefits** — DCC's 27 benefits guides cover CPP, OAS, GIS, CDCP, CRA MyAccount, OHIP+, and provincial programmes. Many eligible seniors are missing benefits they are entitled to simply because they cannot navigate the online application systems. This programme directly addresses that gap.

3. **Building community connections** — Group workshops provide structured social engagement for isolated seniors. Many participants attend as their primary structured weekly social activity. The facilitator-participant relationship creates ongoing touchpoints for community connection.

4. **Supporting lifelong learning** — DCC's 30 self-paced modules allow participants to continue learning independently after the structured programme ends. The platform is free, no-account, and accessible without facilitator support — creating sustainable impact beyond the grant period.

5. **AI literacy for seniors (June 2026 federal priority)** — DCC's AI literacy track aligns directly with ESDC's AI for All strategy, which identifies adults 55+ as a priority group for AI literacy investment. Two Birds Innovation is one of the few community organisations in Ontario with a deployed, accessible AI literacy curriculum for seniors.

## GCOS SECTION F — Sustainability

After the grant period, the programme is sustained through:

1. **Partner venue integration** — Library and community centre partners will incorporate DCC workshops into their regular senior programming at no additional curriculum cost. Two Birds Innovation provides the platform free of charge; venues provide space and volunteer facilitators trained in Year 1.

2. **Train-the-trainer model** — Year 1 trains at least one volunteer co-facilitator per venue. These facilitators can independently deliver all 30 modules after Aaron's facilitation model. No ongoing paid facilitator costs post-Year 1.

3. **Revenue diversification** — Two Birds Innovation offers an optional Partner tier ($500/year) for organisations that want branded onboarding and enhanced support. Year 1 workshops generate awareness of this tier among partner venues.

4. **DCC platform — permanently free** — The DCC platform will remain free-to-access indefinitely. The programme does not depend on continued external funding to operate; NHSP funding covers the deployment and training phase only.
