Canadian Helplines for Seniors

Free phone numbers and online services available to Canadian seniors — for tech help, scam reporting, mental health support, and more. All services listed here are free to call.

🚨 In an emergency, always call 9-1-1. For non-emergency police: call your local police non-emergency line.

Fraud & Scam Reporting

🛡️
Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre
1-888-495-8501
Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4:45 PM ET. Online reporting available 24/7.
Canada's national reporting centre for fraud and cybercrime. Report phone scams, email phishing, romance scams, investment fraud, and identity theft. Even if you did not lose money, your report helps law enforcement find and stop scammers.
🏦
Financial Consumer Agency of Canada (FCAC)
1-866-461-3222
Monday–Friday, 8 AM–6 PM ET
Complaints about banks, financial products, and financial scams. If your bank is not responding to a fraud complaint, the FCAC can help escalate. Also provides free financial literacy resources.
🔐
Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada
1-800-282-1376
Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–4:30 PM ET
If your personal information was stolen, leaked, or misused by a company, you can file a complaint here. Handles privacy breaches involving businesses and federal organisations.

Technology Help

🍎
Apple Support
1-800-275-2273
Available 24/7 for device and software support
Free support for your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple account. You can also visit apple.com/support to chat online or book an appointment at the nearest Apple Store (Genius Bar). Great for issues with your Apple ID, iCloud, or any Apple device.
💻
Computers for Success Canada
1-800-926-8258
Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM
A federal government programme that provides refurbished computers to Canadians who cannot afford them, including seniors on fixed incomes. Also offers basic digital literacy training in many communities.
📚
Your Local Public Library
Call your local branch directly
Hours vary by branch
Most public libraries in Ontario offer free one-on-one technology help, tablet and computer loan programmes, and digital literacy workshops — all free with your library card. Ask at the front desk about 'Digital Help' or 'Tech Tutoring' programmes.

Mental Health & Crisis Support

💙
9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline
9-8-8
Available 24/7, 365 days a year. Call or text.
Canada's national suicide crisis helpline. Free, confidential support in English and French. If you are in distress or worried about someone, you can call or text 9-8-8 from anywhere in Canada.
🧓
Seniors Safety Line (Ontario)
1-866-299-1011
Available 24/7 in 150+ languages
Ontario's free, confidential helpline for seniors experiencing or at risk of abuse, mistreatment, or exploitation — including financial exploitation by family members or scammers. Bilingual and available in over 150 languages.
🤝
Telehealth Ontario
1-866-797-0000
Available 24/7
Free, confidential health advice from a registered nurse — any time of day or night. Get guidance on symptoms, medications, and whether you need to see a doctor. Also available in French.

Government Services & Benefits

🍁
Service Canada — CPP/OAS/GIS Enquiries
1-800-277-9914
Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–4:30 PM local time
Questions about Canada Pension Plan, Old Age Security, and Guaranteed Income Supplement. For questions about your benefits, changes to your address or banking, or delays in payments. The real Service Canada will never call you out of the blue and ask for payment.
🧾
Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Individual Enquiries
1-800-959-8281
Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM local time
Questions about your personal taxes, refunds, RRSP, or benefit programmes. If you receive a suspicious call claiming to be the CRA, do not call back the number they give you — call this number instead to verify. The real CRA never demands gift card payments.
