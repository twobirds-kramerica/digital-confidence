## EMAIL 4 — Day 21: Scam Awareness Reminder

**Subject A:** A warning worth reading: Canadian fraud is up again this year
**Subject B:** One thing that could protect you this week

**Preview text:** The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre reported a record year. Here's what to watch for.

---

Hi [FIRST NAME],

We want to share something important — not to worry you, but because knowing this could protect you.

The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre reported that fraud losses across Canada reached record levels last year. The most common targets? People who receive unexpected messages asking them to click a link, call a number, or share personal information — often pretending to be from a bank, the government, or even a family member.

**The good news: you can protect yourself.**

The three most important things to remember:

1. **Your bank will never ask for your password by email, text, or phone.** Ever.
2. **If something feels rushed or urgent, stop.** Scammers create panic on purpose.
3. **When in doubt, hang up and call the number on the back of your card** — not the one they gave you.

If you haven't completed Module 2 (The Security Shield) yet, this is a great week to do it. It covers all of this in detail, with real examples.

You're learning the right things. Keep going.
