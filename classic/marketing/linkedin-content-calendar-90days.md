## ENGAGEMENT TACTICS

### Comment Strategy
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours on posting day — LinkedIn algorithm rewards early engagement
- Ask a follow-up question in your reply to extend the thread
- If someone shares a strong perspective, write a reply long enough to deserve its own post — then reference their comment in the next one
- Do not just "like" comments — a short genuine response doubles the visibility of that post

### Group Sharing
- Share relevant posts (not all) to: Ontario Tech Community, Credit Union Professional Network, Library and Information Science groups
- Always add a custom note when sharing to a group — don't just cross-post verbatim
- Monthly: share one post to Two Birds Innovation company page with a brief expansion

### Tagging Strategy
- Tag organisations sparingly — only when the content is genuinely relevant to them (2-3 per month max)
- Candidates: CCUA (Canadian Credit Union Association), Ontario Library Association, Age-Friendly Ottawa, CARP
- Never tag an org in a post that could embarrass them or puts them in a difficult position
- Tag individuals (not just orgs) when asking questions — people are more likely to respond than pages

### Best Performing Post Types for the DCC Audience
1. **Specific stories about named seniors (anonymised)** — highest engagement, most shares
2. **Counterintuitive stats** — gets challenged in comments, which drives algorithmic reach
3. **"Here's what I learned" founder reflections** — attracts credit union and library professionals who identify with institution-building
4. **Questions to a specific professional group** — credit union leaders, librarians, PMs — targeted questions get targeted responses
5. **Product decisions explained in plain English** — reaches product community AND validates expertise for B2B prospects

### Posting Rhythm Notes
- Mon 8 AM: Senior Tech or AM topic (people start the week open to ideas)
- Wed 8 AM: AI + Innovation (mid-week, professional mindset, higher reach day)
- Fri 8 AM: Personal or PM wisdom (Friday engagement converts to follows more than other days)
- Avoid: stat-heavy posts on Fridays; long-form storytelling on Mondays
- Boost: repost your highest-performing post from the previous month on a slow week
