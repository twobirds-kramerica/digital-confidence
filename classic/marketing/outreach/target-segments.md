## SEGMENT 1: ONTARIO CREDIT UNIONS (Top 20)

### Key Pain Points
- Fraud claims from senior members who were phished, scammed, or deceived
- High volume of inbound calls for basic digital banking support
- Pressure to grow digital adoption without abandoning older members
- DICO regulatory expectations around member financial literacy
- Competition from big banks that have more digital education resources

### Best Contact Titles
- CEO / President
- VP Member Experience
- VP Operations or VP Digital
- Digital Banking Manager
- Marketing & Communications Director

### Best Approach Channel
First: LinkedIn connection + follow-up message
Second: Direct email to named individual
Third: Phone call (Tuesday–Thursday, 10–11:30 AM)

### Sample Opening Line
"Senior members are your most loyal segment — and the most vulnerable to fraud. I built a platform that helps credit unions protect them."

## SEGMENT 2: ONTARIO PUBLIC LIBRARY SYSTEMS (Top 10)

### Key Pain Points
- Staff overwhelmed by one-on-one digital help requests at reference desks
- Inconsistent digital literacy programming (workshop-dependent, weather-dependent)
- Growing mandate to serve seniors and socially isolated community members
- Pressure to demonstrate community impact for funding renewal
- Limited budget but strong grant-writing capacity

### Best Contact Titles
- CEO / Chief Librarian
- Director of Public Services
- Digital Services Librarian or Digital Literacy Coordinator
- Manager, Community Programming
- Adult Services Librarian

### Best Approach Channel
First: Direct email to named individual (libraries publish staff directories)
Second: LinkedIn
Third: Phone through main branch

### Sample Opening Line
"Your staff are the best digital helpers in town — but they can't follow patrons home. Digital Confidence Centre can."

## SEGMENT 3: ONTARIO HEALTHCARE NETWORKS (Telehealth Module Partners, Top 5)

### Key Pain Points
- Patients 70+ who can't access telehealth portals or video appointments
- Staff time spent helping patients connect to virtual care
- Missed appointments due to digital anxiety
- Provincial mandate to increase virtual care access post-COVID

### Best Contact Titles
- Chief Experience Officer
- Director, Patient Digital Engagement
- VP Innovation or VP Transformation
- Digital Health Manager
- Community Outreach Director

### Best Approach Channel
First: LinkedIn (healthcare executives are active here)
Second: Email (find via organization website)
Partnership framing: Position DCC as a patient enablement tool, not a product sale

### Sample Opening Line
"When a patient can't figure out the telehealth portal, that appointment doesn't happen. Digital Confidence Centre can close that gap."

## SEGMENT 4: MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENT CONTACTS

### Key Pain Points
- Digital services strategy requires residents who can actually use digital services
- Seniors unable to navigate municipal portals (tax payments, permits, transit apps)
- Pressure to demonstrate age-friendly community commitments (WHO Age-Friendly Cities)
- Economic Development officers looking for community wellbeing investments
- Council accountability for digital equity and social inclusion

### Best Contact Titles
- Economic Development Officer (EDO)
- Director of Recreation and Community Services
- Senior Services Coordinator or Age-Friendly Communities Lead
- Chief Administrative Officer (smaller municipalities)
- Library Board Chair or Municipal Councillor (community champion angle)

### Best Approach Channel
First: Direct email via municipal website (addresses are public)
Second: LinkedIn for EDOs and senior services staff
Third: Age-Friendly Communities meetings or local seniors' advisory committee presentations

### Sample Opening Line
"[Municipality Name] has been building age-friendly services for years. Digital Confidence Centre helps make sure seniors can actually use them."

---

### Municipal Approach Strategy

**Tier A — Large City (Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton, London):**
- Target Digital Equity offices or Age-Friendly City coordinators
- Leverage WHO Age-Friendly Cities designation if applicable
- Position as a procurement item under Digital Equity or Community Services budget lines

**Tier B — Mid-Size City (Kitchener, Barrie, Sudbury, Windsor):**
- Target EDO or Recreation/Community Services Director
- Frame around community wellbeing KPIs and strategic plans
- New Horizons grant application support is a strong hook

**Tier C — Small/Rural Municipality (Elgin County, Norfolk County, Grey-Bruce):**
- Direct contact with CAO or Clerk is often appropriate
- Community Development Officer or Age-Friendly Coordinator
- Grant angle is strongest here — budget is tightest, impact is highest
