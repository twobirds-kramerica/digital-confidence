# White-Label Licensing — Digital Confidence Centre

## For Libraries, Credit Unions, Banks & Senior Centres

The Digital Confidence Centre is available as a white-label solution for
Ontario organisations serving seniors. Your organisation gets a fully branded
digital literacy platform — no technical expertise required.

## What They Get

- Branded version of DCC (your logo, colours, typography)
- Custom domain (e.g., digitalliteracy.yourbank.ca)
- Analytics dashboard showing your users only
- Customisable local resources section
- Dedicated support for your staff and users
- Quarterly content updates included

## Pricing Tiers

### Basic — $5,000/year
- Up to 500 active users
- Standard branding (logo + primary colour)
- Monthly analytics PDF report
- Email support (48h response)
- Ideal for: public libraries, community centres

### Professional — $12,000/year
- Up to 2,000 active users
- Full branding customisation (logo, colours, fonts, messaging)
- Real-time analytics dashboard
- 2 custom content modules per year
- Phone + email support (business hours)
- Ideal for: credit unions, regional health units

### Enterprise — $25,000/year
- Unlimited users
- Complete white-label (no DCC branding visible)
- API access for LMS integration
- Custom integrations (member login, CRM)
- Dedicated account manager
- On-site staff training workshops (2/year)
- Ideal for: banks, large hospital systems, insurance providers
