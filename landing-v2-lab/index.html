Why is this free?

The Digital Confidence Centre is offered as a community service by Two Birds Innovation. There is no catch, and here is our promise to you:

  • No account and no signup. You will never be asked for your email or password.
  • No cost, no subscription, and nothing to unlock. Every lesson is open to everyone.
  • No selling. We will never try to sell you anything or share your information.
  • Available in English and French, on any device, anywhere in Canada.
