Password Managers

Bitwarden Password Manager

Free, open-source password manager trusted by millions. Available on iPhone, iPad, and all browsers.

Free (premium $10 USD/year)

Best free option — no personal data sold, no ads

1Password Password Manager

Family-friendly password manager with a clean, easy-to-use design. 14-day free trial available.

From $3.99/month CAD

Recommended by the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security

iCloud Keychain Password Manager

Built into your iPhone and iPad — saves and fills passwords automatically at no cost.

Included with Apple devices

Banking Apps (Canadian Banks)

⚠️ Safety Reminder

Always download your bank's app directly from the App Store by searching your bank's name — never follow a link from an email or text message. Your bank will never send you a link to download their app.

TD Bank App Banking

Manage your TD accounts, check balances, and pay bills securely from your phone.

Free

Search 'TD Bank' in the App Store

Visit TD Bank App →
RBC Mobile Banking

Royal Bank's official mobile app for checking accounts, transferring money, and paying bills.

Free

Search 'RBC Mobile' in the App Store

Visit RBC Mobile →
Interac e-Transfer Banking

Send money securely between Canadian bank accounts — used by all major Canadian banks.

Often included with your bank account

Access through your bank's app — no separate download needed

Learn about Interac →
