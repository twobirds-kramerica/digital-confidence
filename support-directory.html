Canadian Senior Support Directory

Real people and real programmes that help Canadian seniors get confident with technology. Every organisation listed here is free (or free with an Apple device), trusted, and senior-friendly.

✅ You are in a safe place. Every phone number and website on this page has been checked by us.

National Programmes

Free

Connected Canadians

Free one-on-one tech training sessions and digital literacy programmes for Canadian seniors. Friendly, patient volunteers help you with your device at your own pace — no jargon, no rush. You can register for a session using the form on their website.

Phone (toll-free): 1-877-304-5813
Phone (Ottawa local): 613-699-7896
Email: info@connectedcanadians.ca
Website: connectedcanadians.ca
Trusted by thousands of Canadian seniors for friendly, patient tech help

Cyber-Seniors

Volunteer-run tech mentoring that connects seniors with trained student volunteers. Get one-on-one help with your smartphone, tablet, email, video calls, and more.

Phone: 1-844-217-3057
Website: cyberseniors.org
Recommended by libraries and senior centres across Canada

Stand Against Scams

Canada's national campaign to protect Canadians from fraud and scams. Report a scam you've encountered, get prevention tips, and learn how to recognise common tricks before they catch you off guard.

Run by Canada's leading fraud prevention organisations

Free confidential helpline connecting Ontarians to local social and community services, including digital literacy support. Just dial 2-1-1 on any phone — no area code needed.
