What's Coming

This is our public roadmap — a plain-English summary of what we are working on, what is planned, and what we have already finished. We update this page whenever something changes.

Have an idea or a request? Use the Ideas & Feedback link at the bottom of any page to tell us what would help you most. Your suggestions shape what we build next.

What We're Building Next Spring & Summer 2026

Coming Soon

Video Tutorials

Short, clear video walkthroughs for every module — so learners can watch someone do it before trying it themselves. No production jargon, no fast talking, no assumptions. Each video will be captioned and viewable at half speed.

Coming Soon

Print Cheat Sheets

A one-page printable summary for each module — designed to sit beside a device and be referred to in the moment. Large text, simple language, no internet required once printed. Available from the Print Centre.

Coming Soon

More Modules

New modules are in development on topics our learners have asked for: telehealth and virtual doctor appointments, smart home devices, and advanced scam awareness. We currently have 15 modules — more are coming.

Exploring

White-Label Version for Libraries

A customisable version of Digital Confidence Centre for Ontario public libraries, community centres, and credit unions — with their branding, local resources, and a facilitator dashboard. We're talking to partners now.

Quick Wins 1–2 weeks

Quick Win

Location-Specific Resources

Confirm your city so we can show the resources nearest to you — whether you are in St. Thomas, London, Windsor, or another part of Ontario. Resources for your community will appear at the top of the list.

Quick Win

Visual Example Polish

Clearer, easier-to-read formatting for the scam examples shown in Module 2 and the Scam Simulator — so it is even easier to see exactly what warning signs to look for.

Quick Win

More FAQ Answers

We are adding new questions to the FAQ page based on feedback from our beta learners — including questions about Wi-Fi, browser caches, spam folders, and saving passwords safely.

In Progress 2–4 weeks

In Progress

French Language Support

All 11 modules will be available in French for bilingual Ontario seniors. We want every learner to be able to use this site comfortably in the language they feel most confident in.

In Progress

Printable Guides

Download and print any module to keep beside your device. A printed summary is especially helpful for steps you want to follow along with in real life — like setting up passwords or ordering groceries.

In Progress

2FA Training

A clear, step-by-step guide for setting up two-step verification (also called two-factor authentication) on the accounts that matter most — email, banking, and Apple ID. No jargon, just plain steps with screenshots.

Future Ideas Exploring

Future

Practice Exercises

Interactive activities to practise what you learned in a safe environment — no real accounts, no real money, no risk. You can try things out, make mistakes, and learn without any consequences.

Future

Family Progress Dashboard

A simple summary that family members or caregivers can check — showing which modules have been completed and how a loved one is progressing. Designed to be a conversation starter, not a surveillance tool.

Future

Getting Help You Deserve

A new module on dealing with tech support, chatbots, and ageism in customer service — because every person deserves patient, respectful help, and knowing how to ask for it is a skill too.
