## Who DCC Is For

### Primary Learner
**Canadian adults 65+**, primarily on iPhone, iPad, or Android tablet. Low digital confidence — many use their device cautiously or for the first time. They are not technophobes; they are capable people who missed the transition and now feel left behind. Their primary anxiety is "I might break something" or "I might fall for a scam and embarrass myself."

Common starting points: video calling grandchildren, checking CRA / Service Canada benefits, paying a bill, recognising a scam. These are the hooks. Everything else builds from there.

**What success looks like for the learner:** "I opened my CRA account myself. My son didn't have to help me."

### Secondary: The Activator
**Adult children aged 40–60** who set up parents' devices and want them to keep learning independently. They discover DCC, share it, and often monitor progress. They are the viral vector. DCC works for them if it's something they can confidently send to a parent ("Mum, just click this link and follow the steps — it's completely safe").

### Delivery Channel: Library + Community Organisation Facilitators
**Library staff and community workers** running 90-minute group sessions with 8–12 seniors. They need printable handouts, a clear module sequence, and content that works on the projector as well as the learner's own device. They are the B2B customer in the institutional channel.

---

## The Problem We Solve

**Three problems, in priority order:**

1. **Fear.** Seniors avoid the digital world because they are afraid of doing something irreversible. Every new task feels risky. DCC's job is to remove fear before teaching skill.

2. **Inaccessibility.** Every existing resource is either too young (Be Internet Awesome is K–8), too American (AARP), too dense (government PDF guides), or too thin (one-page articles). There is no Canadian, free, comprehensive, senior-first, anxiety-aware learning programme.

3. **Isolation dependency.** Seniors currently depend on family or phone-support staff for every digital task. DCC's endgame is independence — the ability to solve a new problem using patterns learned in the course, not needing to call someone.

## The Sales Model (Current + Future)

### Free (Always)
The core 29-module programme is permanently free. No login, no paywall, no tracking. This is the public-good foundation and the distribution engine. "Free, no signup, works on any device" is the message that removes friction and generates word-of-mouth referrals among the activator audience.

### B2B Institutional Channel
Libraries, community health centres, seniors' centres, school boards (digital literacy for adult learners), and employer onboarding (older workforce, TELUS, government departments). The institutional pitch is:

> DCC is a ready-made, AODA-compliant digital literacy programme you can run tomorrow. No setup, no software, no licence fee for the base content. White-label licensing available for organisations that want to brand it as their own.

Revenue model options (for Aaron's decision):
- **Usage-based licensing** — organisation pays a flat annual fee for facilitator training, printable workbooks, and branding customisation
- **Facilitator certification** — DCC-certified facilitator programme (train-the-trainer); organisations send staff, pay per cohort
- **Grant-funded deployment** — apply with libraries or community health centres for accessibility/digital-literacy grant funding; DCC provides the programme, org provides the venue and facilitator

### Potential Future Channels
- **Telecom partnerships** (TELUS, Bell, Rogers) — include DCC as a value-add for senior device plan customers
- **CRA / Service Canada partnership** — government digital-access initiatives; DCC as a recommended resource
- **Insurance / benefits providers** — digital literacy as a member benefit for senior health plan holders

|---|---|
| Government PDF guides | Too long, not interactive, not anxiety-aware |
| AARP (US) | Wrong country, member-gated, magazine-style overwhelm |

| GCF LearnFree | No Canadian content, no audio read-aloud, no senior-first design |
| Library workshops | One-time, no take-home reference, not self-paced |
| YouTube tutorials | Not linear, overwhelming choice, not reassurance-first |

**DCC's unique combination:** Canadian + Free + No login + Audio read-aloud + AODA-compliant + Senior-first anxiety design + Comprehensive (29 modules, not 3 articles) + Institutionally deployable.
