# The Home Button: Your Safety Net
**Module:** Module 1: Mastering the Escape Hatch
**Duration Target:** 3-4 minutes
**Audience:** Ontario seniors aged 70+

---

## SCRIPT

**[OPEN]**
[NARRATOR] Welcome back. You're doing great.

**[VISUAL CUE: Warm title card — "The Home Button: Your Safety Net"]**
[NARRATOR] Today we're going to talk about one of the most important things on your iPad — the Home button. Or on newer iPads, the gesture that does the same job. Think of it as your safety net. No matter what happens on your screen, this one move brings you back to somewhere familiar.

**[VISUAL CUE: Show an older iPad with a physical Home button at the bottom]**
[NARRATOR] If you have an older iPad — one with a round button at the bottom of the screen — that is your Home button. Go ahead and look at your iPad right now. Can you see it? That button is your best friend.

**[VISUAL CUE: Show a newer iPad without a physical Home button]**
[NARRATOR] If you have a newer iPad without that round button, don't worry. You use a swipe instead. You simply put your finger at the very bottom of the screen and swipe upward — like you're lifting a curtain. That does the same thing.

**[VISUAL CUE: Demonstration — pressing Home button, screen returns to home screen]**
[NARRATOR] Let's see what happens when you press or swipe home. You're in the middle of something. Maybe a website, maybe an app, maybe something you've accidentally opened. You press the Home button — and there you are. Back on your home screen. Back in familiar territory.

**[VISUAL CUE: Show a jumbled-looking screen, then press Home — clean home screen appears]**
[NARRATOR] Nothing bad happened. Nothing was deleted. Whatever you were looking at is still there if you want to go back. But now you're safe. The Home button doesn't break anything. It doesn't erase anything. It simply takes you home.

**[VISUAL CUE: Close-up of the home screen grid of app icons]**
[NARRATOR] Your home screen is like the front door of your house. It's where everything starts. If you ever feel confused, overwhelmed, or unsure — press Home first. Take a breath. Then decide what to do next.

**[VISUAL CUE: Demonstrate opening an app — Safari — then pressing Home]**
[NARRATOR] Let's try it together. I'm going to open an app — this is Safari, the web browser. Now I'm in the browser. Let's say something strange appeared on the screen. I don't know what it is. I press Home. And I'm back. Safe. Simple.

**[VISUAL CUE: Show the swipe-up gesture on a newer iPad in slow motion]**
[NARRATOR] If you're on a newer iPad, here is the swipe. You start at the very bottom edge of the screen — right at the frame — and you gently swipe upward, about halfway up the screen. Not too fast, not too slow. Give it a try whenever you feel ready.

**[VISUAL CUE: Show text on screen: "Remember: Home never hurts"]**
[NARRATOR] Here is the key thing to remember: pressing Home never hurts. It never deletes anything. It never sends anything. It never makes a purchase. It is always safe to press. If you are ever unsure what to do on your iPad — press Home first.

**[VISUAL CUE: Show a senior smiling and using an iPad confidently]**
[NARRATOR] With just this one skill, you already have more control than you might think. You have an escape route. Wherever you end up, you can always get back.

**[CLOSE]**
[NARRATOR] Remember the 3-Second Rule: if something feels wrong, stop, breathe, and wait three seconds before tapping anything. You can always come back to something, but you can't un-tap a link. See you in the next video.
