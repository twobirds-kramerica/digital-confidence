## Video 1 of 3: Ordering Groceries Online
**Target Duration:** 8 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner

### HOOK (First 15 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "Imagine never having to push a heavy cart through a crowded store on a cold February day, never struggling to reach the top shelf, and never getting home only to realise you forgot the one thing you went for. Online grocery shopping can genuinely change the experience of grocery shopping — and today we'll show you how it works."

### INTRODUCTION (30 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "Welcome to Module 10 of the Digital Confidence Centre. This module covers grocery and food delivery on your iPad — ordering groceries for delivery or pick-up, using food delivery apps for prepared meals, and finding the best deals. In this first video, we'll walk through online grocery ordering from start to finish."
[VISUAL CUE]: Grocery bag with delivery icon. Title card.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Module 10 · Video 1 of 3: Ordering Groceries Online

---

### MAIN CONTENT

**SEGMENT 1: Which Grocery Services Are Available in Ontario?**
[VOICEOVER]: "Several major grocery chains in Ontario offer online ordering with either home delivery or curbside pick-up. Sobeys and FreshCo offer online ordering through the Voilà by Sobeys service. Loblaws, No Frills, Zehrs, and Valu-Mart are all part of the PC Express system — one account works for all of them. Walmart Canada also offers grocery delivery and pick-up. Instacart is a third-party service that shops at multiple stores including Costco, LCBO, and Shoppers Drug Mart. We'll use PC Express as our example today since it's available across most of Ontario."
[VISUAL CUE]: Map of Ontario with grocery chain logos. PC Express, Voilà, Walmart, and Instacart logos shown.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Ontario grocery online: PC Express · Voilà by Sobeys · Walmart · Instacart

**SEGMENT 2: Creating an Account and Choosing Delivery vs. Pick-Up**
[VOICEOVER]: "Open the App Store and download the PC Express app — or go to pcexpress.ca in Safari. Create a free account with your email address. Once registered, you'll be asked to choose your store and whether you want delivery to your home or curbside pick-up — where you drive to the store and your order is brought out to your car. Pick-up usually has a lower fee and more available time slots. Delivery is more convenient if you can't drive. Choose the option that works for your situation."
[VISUAL CUE]: PC Express app → account creation → store selection → delivery vs pick-up choice screen.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Choose: home delivery (more convenient) or curbside pick-up (lower fee, more time slots)

**SEGMENT 3: Building Your Cart**
[VOICEOVER]: "Once you've chosen your store and time slot, you can start shopping. Use the search bar to find specific items, or browse categories like Produce, Dairy, or Bakery. Tap any item to see its details and price. Tap the plus icon to add it to your cart. Your cart total updates in real time at the bottom of the screen. If you shop regularly, look for a 'Your Frequently Bought Items' or 'My Favourites' list — most services remember what you've ordered before and show it prominently, making repeat orders very fast."
[VISUAL CUE]: PC Express app → search bar → item found → plus button → cart total updating. Favourites list highlighted.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Search item → tap plus to add to cart · Your Favourites makes repeat orders fast

**SEGMENT 4: Choosing Substitutions**
[VOICEOVER]: "A key setting in grocery delivery is your substitution preference. Because stores run out of items, most services will offer you a substitution — a similar item if your first choice is unavailable. You can choose to accept substitutions (the store picks a similar item), decline substitutions (you'd rather not receive the item at all), or pick your own substitutions for key items. Accepting substitutions generally means fewer missing items in your order — and you can reject the substitution at delivery if you don't want it."
[VISUAL CUE]: Substitution settings page shown. Item detail page with "My Substitution" field.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Substitutions: accept = fewer missing items · You can decline at delivery if unwanted

**SEGMENT 5: Checking Out and Tracking Your Order**
[VOICEOVER]: "When your cart is ready, tap the cart icon and then Checkout. You'll review your items, see the subtotal plus any delivery fee and tip (for delivery orders), and enter or confirm your payment method. Most services accept credit and debit cards. After confirming, you'll receive an order confirmation email. For delivery orders, you'll receive updates as your order is prepared and a notification when your driver is on the way, with a live map showing their location. For pick-up, you'll get a notification when your order is ready and instructions on where to pull up."
[VISUAL CUE]: Cart → Checkout → order review → payment → confirmed. Order status notifications shown.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Checkout → review → pay → track your delivery in real time

---

### COMMON MISTAKES (1 minute)
[VOICEOVER]: "Two grocery delivery pitfalls. First: choosing a delivery window that's too soon. Building a large cart can take 20 to 30 minutes for a first-timer. Choose a delivery slot at least a couple of hours away. Second: not checking the order minimum. Many delivery services have a minimum order value — often $35 to $50 — before they'll deliver. If your cart is under the minimum, you'll be prompted to add more before you can check out."
[VISUAL CUE]: Minimum order graphic — cart total under minimum with prompt. Time slot selection screen showing available windows.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Check the minimum order (usually $35–$50) and choose a time slot with enough time

---

### RECAP (30 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "Today's key points. One: PC Express, Voilà, Walmart, and Instacart all serve most of Ontario. Two: choose delivery or curbside pick-up at account setup. Three: use the Favourites list to speed up repeat orders. Four: set your substitution preferences. Five: track your delivery in real time after checkout."
[VISUAL CUE]: Five-point recap list.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Choose service → pick-up or delivery → cart → substitutions → checkout → track

## Video 2 of 3: Food Delivery Apps — Meals to Your Door
**Target Duration:** 7 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner

### HOOK (First 15 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "Whether it's a night when cooking isn't in the cards, a special occasion where you want your favourite restaurant meal without the drive, or simply a Tim Hortons coffee delivered to your door on a snowy morning — food delivery apps make it remarkably convenient. Today we'll walk you through exactly how they work."

### INTRODUCTION (30 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "In this video, we'll cover the major food delivery apps available in Ontario — DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Skip the Dishes — how to place an order, how to track it, and how to tip appropriately. We'll use DoorDash as our main example."
[VISUAL CUE]: DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Skip the Dishes logos. Food delivery graphic. Title card.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Module 10 · Video 2 of 3: Food Delivery Apps

---

### MAIN CONTENT

**SEGMENT 1: Downloading and Setting Up DoorDash**
[VOICEOVER]: "Search for 'DoorDash' in the App Store and download the free app. Open it and create an account with your email address, or sign in with your Apple ID. Enter your delivery address — your home address — and you'll see all the restaurants and stores delivering to your area. The home screen shows categories: Fast Food, Comfort Food, Pizza, Healthy, Convenience Stores. Scroll through or use the search bar at the top to find a specific restaurant."
[VISUAL CUE]: App Store → DoorDash download → address entry → home screen categories.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: DoorDash: download free → enter your address → browse restaurants near you

**SEGMENT 2: Placing an Order**
[VOICEOVER]: "Tap a restaurant to see their menu. Browse the categories — appetisers, mains, drinks, sides. Tap any item to see a photo, description, and price. Customise if required — sizes, add-ons. Tap 'Add to Order.' When you're ready to check out, tap the red bar at the bottom showing your cart total. You'll see your items, the delivery fee, a service fee, and an estimated delivery time. Add a tip for your driver — 15 to 20 percent is standard, and drivers rely on tips. Enter your payment method and tap Place Order."
[VISUAL CUE]: Restaurant menu → item selected → customised → added → checkout screen with all fees shown → Place Order.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Review all fees at checkout · Tip your driver: 15–20% is standard and appreciated

**SEGMENT 3: Tracking Your Order**
[VOICEOVER]: "After placing your order, a tracking screen appears showing the stages: restaurant received your order, restaurant is preparing your food, driver picked up your order, driver is on the way. A live map shows where your driver is in real time. You'll receive a push notification when your driver is nearby and again when they arrive. Most orders arrive within 30 to 45 minutes. If there's a significant delay, the app will notify you and show the updated estimated time."
[VISUAL CUE]: Order tracking stages shown in sequence. Live map with driver location dot moving toward delivery address.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Live tracking: restaurant preparing → driver picked up → driver on the way → arrived

**SEGMENT 4: Skip the Dishes and Uber Eats**
[VOICEOVER]: "Skip the Dishes is a Canadian-founded food delivery service — available across Ontario and beloved in smaller communities where DoorDash and Uber Eats may have fewer restaurant options. It works the same way: download the app, enter your address, choose a restaurant, add to cart, check out. Uber Eats is owned by Uber — if you already have an Uber account from Module 11 on ride-sharing, the same account works. Prices and restaurant selection vary between services, so it's worth checking a couple to compare for your specific location."
[VISUAL CUE]: Skip the Dishes app icon and interface briefly shown. Uber Eats shown. Same-account note for Uber.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Skip the Dishes: great for smaller Ontario cities · Uber Eats: same account as Uber rides

**SEGMENT 5: The Tim Hortons App and Other Brand Apps**
[VOICEOVER]: "Many chains have their own apps that often offer better deals than third-party delivery services. The Tim Hortons app lets you order ahead for pick-up — your order is ready when you arrive, no waiting in line — and you earn Tim Hortons Rewards points on every purchase. The McDonald's app and Harvey's app work similarly. For coffee and quick-service meals, ordering directly through the brand's own app is usually cheaper than DoorDash or Skip, because you're not paying the delivery service's added fees."
[VISUAL CUE]: Tim Hortons app icon. Mobile ordering screen shown. Rewards points accruing.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Tim Hortons app: order ahead, skip the line, earn rewards — often cheaper than delivery apps

### COMMON MISTAKES (1 minute)
[VOICEOVER]: "Two delivery mistakes. First: not checking the estimated delivery time before ordering. During busy periods — Friday evenings, lunch on weekdays — delivery estimates can be 60 to 90 minutes. If you're hungry now, pick a restaurant with a shorter estimate, or use the pick-up option to collect yourself. Second: not checking your order when it arrives. Occasionally items are missing or wrong. Check immediately while the driver is still nearby — the app has a 'Help' option for reporting issues with your order, and a refund or replacement is usually issued quickly."
[VISUAL CUE]: Long delivery estimate shown on restaurant card. Order receipt check — finding a missing item and using Help in app.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Check estimated time before ordering · Check your order when it arrives — report issues in the app

Three: tip your driver 15 to 20 percent. Four: track your order live in the app. Five: brand apps like Tim Hortons often offer better deals for pick-up orders."
[VISUAL CUE]: Five-point recap list.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Choose app for your area · Check fees · Tip driver · Track live · Brand apps = better deals

## Video 3 of 3: Getting the Best Deals on Grocery and Food Delivery
**Target Duration:** 6 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner

### HOOK (First 15 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "Grocery and food delivery is convenient — but delivery fees, service charges, and tips can add up quickly. The good news is that with a few smart habits, you can significantly reduce what you pay. Today we'll go through every deal-finding strategy worth knowing."

### INTRODUCTION (30 seconds)
[VOICEOVER]: "In this final video of Module 10, we'll cover grocery loyalty apps — especially PC Optimum — food delivery discount codes, the best times to order for lower fees, and a few other money-saving tips for online food and grocery shopping."
[VISUAL CUE]: PC Optimum logo. Discount coupon graphic. Title card.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Module 10 · Video 3 of 3: Getting the Best Deals

---

### MAIN CONTENT

**SEGMENT 1: PC Optimum — Ontario's Most Valuable Loyalty Program**
[VOICEOVER]: "If you shop at any Loblaw-owned store — Loblaws, No Frills, Zehrs, Valu-Mart, Shoppers Drug Mart — the PC Optimum app is the single best deal-finding tool available to you. Download it free, create an account, and link your PC Optimum card. Every purchase earns points. The app shows personalised offers — often extra points on items you already buy. When you have enough points, redeem them for free groceries. Many members earn $20 to $50 in free groceries every few months just from regular shopping."
[VISUAL CUE]: PC Optimum app → personalised offers shown → points accumulating → redeem for groceries.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: PC Optimum: free to join · personalised offers · points = free groceries

**SEGMENT 2: Grocery Deal Apps — Flipp and Reebee**
[VOICEOVER]: "Flipp and Reebee are free apps that compile digital flyers from all the major grocery stores in your area. Instead of sorting through paper flyers, you see all the weekly deals in one place. Enter your postal code and you'll see this week's sales at your local stores — you can even make a shopping list from the deals and the app will sort the list by store. These apps make it easy to see which store has the best price on chicken this week or whether Shoppers or No Frills has your preferred items on sale."
[VISUAL CUE]: Flipp app showing multiple store flyers. Shopping list built from sales items. Postal code entry.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Flipp or Reebee: all grocery flyers in one app · Build your shopping list from the deals

**SEGMENT 3: Reducing Food Delivery Fees**
[VOICEOVER]: "Food delivery fees can be substantial — a $15 meal can cost $25 after fees and tip. Here are ways to reduce them. First: DashPass on DoorDash and Uber One on Uber Eats are subscription services — roughly $10 a month — that give you free delivery on orders over a minimum. If you order delivery more than twice a month, these subscriptions save money. Second: look for 'Free Delivery' filters in the app — some restaurants offer this permanently. Third: order directly from the restaurant's own app if they offer it — many charge no delivery fee for self-ordering."
[VISUAL CUE]: DashPass subscription vs per-order fees comparison. Free Delivery filter in DoorDash app. Restaurant's own app vs delivery app pricing.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: DashPass / Uber One: ~$10/month = free delivery if you order twice+ per month

**SEGMENT 4: Promo Codes and First-Time Discounts**
[VOICEOVER]: "Most delivery apps offer significant discounts to new users — often 30 to 50 percent off your first few orders. If someone in your household hasn't tried DoorDash yet, their first order can be substantially discounted. Referral codes — shared by friends or family who already use the service — also provide discounts. Check the Promotions or Offers section of any delivery app before ordering — there are often limited-time deals on specific restaurants or free delivery on certain days."
[VISUAL CUE]: DoorDash promo code entry field. Promotions tab highlighted in app. First-order discount graphic.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: New user? Look for 50% off first order · Check Promotions tab before every order

**SEGMENT 5: Curbside Pick-Up Saves the Most**
[VOICEOVER]: "If saving money is the priority, curbside pick-up is almost always the best option. Most grocery services charge no pick-up fee, compared to $5 to $10 for delivery. You still don't have to walk through the store or load heavy bags — you just drive to the designated spot and staff load your car. For those who don't drive, consider asking a family member or neighbour to do the pick-up run — you can do all the ordering yourself on your iPad and the other person just picks it up."
[VISUAL CUE]: Curbside pick-up graphic — car at grocery store, staff loading. Fee comparison: $0 pick-up vs $8 delivery.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Curbside pick-up: usually free, no heavy lifting — best value option

---

### COMMON MISTAKES (1 minute)
[VOICEOVER]: "One mistake that costs people money: forgetting that promotional pricing is temporary. A delivery subscription that's free for the first month will automatically charge your card when the free period ends. Always note when any free trial ends and decide in advance whether you want to continue. Check your subscriptions monthly in Settings — your name — Subscriptions, as we covered in Module 4."
[VISUAL CUE]: Subscription free trial → charge starting. Settings → Subscriptions reminder.
[ON-SCREEN TEXT]: Set a reminder before any free trial ends — check Settings → your name → Subscriptions
