Module 1: Mastering the Escape Hatch

What you will learn: The single most important skill in using any device — how to get back to safety, no matter what happens on your screen.

This takes about 20 to 30 minutes. Go at your own pace. Nothing on this page can harm your device. You cannot break anything by reading.

Margaret's Story

Margaret, 74, was browsing recipes on her iPad one Tuesday morning. She tapped on a link, and suddenly her screen was filled with flashing colours and a loud voice saying her iPad had a virus. Her heart raced. She thought her iPad was broken — or worse.

Here is the good news: Margaret's iPad was perfectly fine the entire time. That scary message was a fake — a common trick designed to frighten people.

Tell me more about what happened to Margaret

Margaret did what many of us would do: she panicked. She pressed every button she could find. Nothing seemed to work. Finally, she unplugged her iPad and hid it in a drawer for three days.

If Margaret had known the one skill we are about to learn, she could have closed that fake warning in about two seconds and gone right back to her butter tart recipe. That skill is what we call the Escape Hatch.

The Golden Rule: Nothing Is Permanent

You cannot break your iPad by tapping the wrong thing. You cannot accidentally delete the internet. You cannot "ruin" your device by opening the wrong app.

Almost everything you do on your iPad can be undone, closed, or fixed.

Tell me more — why is it so forgiving?

Think of your iPad like a library. When you open an app, it is like picking a book off the shelf. When you close the app, you are putting the book back. The book is still there. The library is still there. You have not changed anything permanent.

Even if you accidentally delete an app, you can get it back. Even if you type something in the wrong place, you can erase it. Even if a strange website pops up on your screen, you can close it. Your iPad is remarkably forgiving.

Your Three Escape Hatches

There are three ways to get back to safety from anywhere on your device. You only need to learn one to feel in control — the other two are bonuses.

The Home button is your best friend. No matter how lost or confused you feel, pressing it always takes you back to familiar ground. You cannot break anything by pressing it, so press it as often as you like.

Escape Hatch #1: The Home Button

On older iPads, look at the very bottom edge, below the screen. You will see a round, slightly indented circle. That is your Home button.

Press it once with your thumb or finger. Not hard — a gentle, firm press. Whatever was on your screen disappears, and you are back at your Home screen.

Tell me more — what if I have a newer iPad?

Newer iPads do not have a round button. Instead, look at the very bottom edge of the screen. You will see a thin, dark horizontal line.

Place your finger on that line and swipe upward — slide your finger from the bottom of the screen toward the middle. You are back at your Home screen. Safe and sound.
