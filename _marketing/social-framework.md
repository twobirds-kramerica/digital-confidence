## Content Pillars

### 1. Quick Tips (40% of posts)
Short, actionable advice seniors can use today.

**Format:** 1–3 sentences + clear image or graphic
**Frequency:** 3× per week
**Examples:**
- "Did you know you can make text bigger on your iPhone? Go to Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size and drag the slider. Your eyes will thank you."
- "Before clicking any link in an email, look at who sent it. If the address after the @ looks strange — delete the email."
- "Siri can read the news aloud. Just say: 'Hey Siri, what's in the news today?'"

---

### 2. Stories of Confidence (25% of posts)
Real-feeling scenarios showing seniors overcoming a digital challenge.

**Format:** 2–4 sentences, first person, relatable scenario
**Frequency:** 1–2× per week
**Tone:** Warm, empowering, never condescending
**Examples:**
- "Margaret from St. Thomas used to call her son every time she got a pop-up on her screen. After Module 2, she knew exactly what it was — and what to do. She called him to share the good news instead."
- "Frank didn't think video calls were for him. His granddaughter walked him through FaceTime on a Sunday afternoon. Now they do a weekly 'coffee call' every Thursday."

---

### 3. Module Spotlights (20% of posts)
Feature a specific module or lesson to drive course engagement.

**Format:** Module name + key takeaway + CTA link
**Frequency:** 1× per week
**Examples:**
- "📱 Module 3: Passwords & Biometrics is live. Did you know your fingerprint is actually the most secure password you have? Free lesson — link in bio."
- "🏥 Module 15: Telehealth. Never been on a video call with a doctor? This lesson walks you through it, step by step, on your iPad. Takes about 30 minutes."

---

### 4. Community & Partners (10% of posts)
Highlight local organizations, events, and B2B partnerships.

**Format:** Partner name + what they do + connection to DCC
**Frequency:** 2–3× per month
**Examples:**
- "Connected Canadians offers free 1-on-1 tech support for seniors across Ontario. Call them at 1-855-808-0505. We recommend them to every DCC learner."
- "Did you know the St. Thomas Public Library runs free digital literacy drop-ins? Pair them with your DCC modules for the full experience."

---

### 5. Behind the Scenes / Trust-Building (5% of posts)
Build transparency and connection with the DCC team.

**Format:** Personal tone, no jargon
**Frequency:** 1–2× per month
**Examples:**
- "We tested every lesson with real seniors before publishing it. If something was confusing, we rewrote it. Our goal: zero 'I don't understand' moments."
- "DCC is built by Two Birds Innovation, a small Ontario team. We believe every Canadian senior deserves to feel confident with technology."

---

## Voice and Tone Guide

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Warm, patient, encouraging | Condescending or "dumbed down" |
| Plain language (Grade 7–8 reading level) | Jargon ("UI", "bandwidth", "two-factor") without explanation |
| Specific and actionable | Vague ("technology is important!") |
| Ontario / Canadian context | American references (US phone numbers, IRS, Medicare) |
| First person singular ("you can do this") | Collective generalising ("seniors often feel…") |
| Celebrate small wins | Emphasise difficulty or fear |
| Canadian English spelling | American English spelling |

## Content Calendar Template (Monthly)

### Week 1 — Foundation Focus
- Introduce a core safety module (1, 2, or 3)
- Quick tip: basic device skill
- Story: fear → confidence arc

### Week 2 — Community Focus
- Partner spotlight (library, Connected Canadians, etc.)
- Quick tip: communication skill (video calls, messaging)
- Module spotlight: staying connected

### Week 3 — Practical Skills
- Financial safety or health technology module
- Quick tip: everyday task (photos, weather, timer)
- Story: independence theme

### Week 4 — New Content / Seasonal
- Promote any new module or resource
- Seasonal tip (tax season, travel, holiday shopping)
- Reflection / community thank-you post

## Image and Graphic Guidelines

- Use warm, authentic photos of real older adults (not stock photos that look staged)
- Avoid photos of confused or frustrated people with technology
- Text overlays: minimum 24pt font, high contrast (white text on dark background or vice versa)
- Preferred colour palette: DCC blue (#1565C0), warm gold, white
- Always include alt text on every image for accessibility
- No images of faces without consent

**Free image sources:**
- Unsplash (search: "senior technology", "older adult tablet")
- Pexels (same search terms)
- DCC's own user photos (with consent)

## Engagement Protocol

**Respond to all comments within 24 hours (business days)**

| Comment Type | Response |
|-------------|----------|
| Question about a module | Answer + link to the relevant lesson |
| Technical problem | Acknowledge + suggest Connected Canadians 1-855-808-0505 |
| Positive feedback | Thank them warmly + tag relevant module |
| Negative/complaint | Acknowledge privately, offer direct support |
| Spam or scam | Delete + report, do not engage |

## Emergency / Rapid Response Posts

When a new scam targeting Canadian seniors is reported (CAFC alert, media coverage):

1. Post warning within 24 hours of alert
2. Format: what the scam is + one clear warning sign + what to do if targeted
3. Always include CAFC number: 1-888-495-8501
4. Link to DCC scam simulator or Module 2 if relevant
5. Tag: `#ScamAlert` `#CAFCAlert` `#OntarioSeniors`
