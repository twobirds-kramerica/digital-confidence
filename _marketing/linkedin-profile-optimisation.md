## Additional Profile Recommendations

### Profile Photo
Use a professional but approachable headshot. No suit jacket — button-up or smart casual. St. Thomas or outdoor Ontario backdrop if possible. LinkedIn profiles with photos get 21x more views.

### Banner Image
Create a simple banner with:
- "Two Birds Innovation" logo or wordmark
- Tagline: "AI Strategy for Ontario SMEs"
- Location: "St. Thomas, Ontario"
- Keep it clean — dark background, white text, one accent colour

- Comment on 2–3 posts per day from Ontario business leaders, AI practitioners, or local economic development accounts
- Share (with commentary) one relevant article per week — AI adoption, Ontario economic news, digital literacy topics
- Respond to every comment on your own posts within 12 hours
