# DCC Library Pilot Runbook
**Version:** 1.0 · **Created:** 2026-06-10 (BULL SPRINT 1 Tier 3 deliverable)
**Purpose:** Aaron's reference for running a library pilot — from first "yes" to 6-week completion

---

## What a Library Pilot Is

A free 6-week engagement with one library. Three patrons. One facilitator (usually a library staff member). Aaron provides setup support and a debrief at end of week 6. No cost to the library.

**Goal of the pilot:** Generate a named case study and a reference contact Aaron can share with other libraries. Revenue is not the goal of the first pilot.

---

## Phase 1: From Outreach to "Yes" (Days 1-7)

### What Aaron sends
**Subject line:** Free digital literacy curriculum for seniors — pilot offer

**Body (template):**
"Hi [Name], I built a free digital literacy curriculum for seniors that addresses the gap left by the federal Digital Literacy Exchange Program ending in March 2025. The library version includes a facilitator guide and works on any device with a browser. I am looking for one library to run a free 6-week pilot with three patrons. I will provide the setup and a debrief call at the end. Would you have 20 minutes to see what it looks like? [Calendly link]"

**Page to share:** https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca/for-libraries.html

### On the discovery call (30 min)
1. Ask: "Who do you have in mind for the three patrons? Are they already library card holders?"
2. Ask: "Does your library have a computer access program or drop-in program we could attach this to?"
3. Ask: "What's your preferred method for communicating with patrons about this?"
4. Confirm: You will send the facilitator guide and a setup checklist within 48 hours.

### After the call
- Email the facilitator guide: https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca/facilitator-guide.html
- Set a check-in call for end of Week 2

## Phase 2: Setup Week (Days 7-14)

### What the facilitator needs to do
1. Identify 3 patrons to invite (ideally: 1 complete beginner, 1 with basic smartphone, 1 who uses email already)
2. Book 3 sessions (1.5 hours each, once per week for 6 weeks)
3. Tell patrons the session URL: https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca (they start at Module 1)

### What Aaron provides
- Facilitator guide link: https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca/facilitator-guide.html
- Suggested session structure: Module 1-2 in Session 1, Module 3-4 in Session 2, etc.
- Hotline: aaron.patzalek@gmail.com for any questions the facilitator has

### Setup checklist (send to facilitator)
- [ ] Three patrons identified and invited
- [ ] Device available for each patron (library computer or personal)
- [ ] Browser confirmed (Chrome or Firefox — not IE)
- [ ] Module 1 loaded and tested on at least one device
- [ ] First session scheduled

---

## Phase 3: Six-Week Session Guide

### Session structure (per session)
- **5 min:** Welcome, check in ("did anything change with your phone/device since last time?")
- **50 min:** Work through 2 modules (patron-paced; facilitator available for questions)
- **5 min:** Recap ("what was the one thing you'll use from today?")

### Module schedule (suggested)
| Session | Modules | Theme |
|---------|---------|-------|
| 1 | 1-2 | Getting started / basics |
| 2 | 3-4 | Safety + daily use |
| 3 | 5-6 | Communication |
| 4 | 7-8 | Photos + staying in touch |
| 5 | 9-10 | Finding information + AI basics |
| 6 | Catch-up + Module 22 (subscription traps) | Review |

### If a patron misses a session
The modules are designed to be self-paced. The patron can work through missed modules at home before the next session. Nothing is lost if they miss a week.

---

## Phase 4: Mid-Pilot Check-In (End of Week 2)

### Aaron's 15-minute call with the facilitator
1. Ask: "Has anything been confusing for patrons?"
2. Ask: "Is the pacing right, or are they going faster/slower than expected?"
3. Ask: "Any technical issues with the modules?" (Report to aaron.patzalek@gmail.com if yes)
4. Ask: "Any feedback on what they wish was different?"

---

## Phase 5: Pilot Completion (Week 6)

### Certificate
If any patron completes all modules, they can receive a completion certificate at: https://digitalconfidencecentre.ca/certificate.html

The digital certificate is free. The official CA$9.99 printed certificate is available after Aaron completes Stripe KYC.

### Debrief call with director/facilitator (30 min)
1. Ask: "What was the most valuable part for patrons?"
2. Ask: "What would you change?"
3. Ask: "Would you recommend this to other libraries?"
4. Ask: "Would you be willing to be named in our pilot case study?"

**If they say yes to the case study:** Ask permission to use library name + city + a one-sentence quote.

### Case study template
> "[Library name] ([City], Ontario) ran a 6-week digital literacy pilot with 3 senior patrons using the Digital Confidence Centre curriculum. [Quote from facilitator or patron]. Contact: [Name] at [email]."

## Common Questions From Library Directors

**Q: Is there a cost?**
The curriculum is free for patrons and libraries. A CA$500/year site licence is available for libraries that want a dedicated patron portal, priority support, and facilitator training sessions.

**Q: Is it available in French?**
Bilingual English/French support is built in. Patrons can switch languages from any module page.

**Q: What happens when we run out of modules?**
New modules are added regularly. Libraries can repeat the curriculum with new patron cohorts.

**Q: Is it AODA-compliant?**
The curriculum meets WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards. Large text, high contrast, and screen reader support are built in.
