Give Your Parent the Digital Confidence They Deserve

You can't always be there when the scary pop-up appears. DCC Family gives the adults in your life a guided, structured path through the digital world — at their own pace, with your support from a distance.

Sound Familiar?

📞

The Sunday call

Your parent calls every Sunday with a new technology panic. You love them, but you've explained the Home button four times this month.

😟

The scam worry

You lie awake worrying they'll click a phishing link or give their banking details to someone who calls pretending to be the CRA.

📍

The distance problem

You live an hour away — or an ocean away. You can't just pop over to fix things. They need to be able to handle it themselves.

💻

The video call gap

They have an iPad. They have grandchildren who want to video call. But getting them set up and confident takes more patience than either of you has on a weeknight.

What Your Parent Gets

DCC is 29 self-paced modules built specifically for Canadian seniors. Every module follows the same warm, reassuring structure. No jargon. No rush. No failing.

  • 1
    The Escape Hatch — the one skill that changes everything

    Before anything else, they learn how to get back to safety on any device, no matter what appears on the screen. This alone removes 80% of the panic calls.

  • 2
    Scam recognition — the CRA call, the fake pop-up, the urgent text

    Canada-specific scenarios. Real examples. Your parent will recognise the exact scams that target Canadian seniors in 2026.

  • 3
    Banking and passwords — safely, confidently

    They learn how to use online banking, create strong passwords, and why two-factor authentication is their friend, not an obstacle.

  • 4
    Video calls, photos, and staying connected

    The reason they got the device in the first place. By Module 8, they're initiating the video calls instead of waiting for you to call them.

  • 5
    AI, smart home, and what's actually coming

    Modules 9–21 cover the digital world as it is now — not as it was ten years ago. Your parent won't feel left behind.
