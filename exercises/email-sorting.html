Practice Exercise: Sorting Your Inbox

Drag each email into the correct folder: Keep in Inbox, Move to Spam, or Delete Immediately. Look closely at the sender address — scammers often use addresses that look almost right.

Emails to sort — drag each one into a folder below:

From: newsletter@libraryontario.ca
Your library card renewal is ready
From: security-alert@apple-id-verify.net
Your Apple ID has been suspended
From: family@gmail.com
Photos from Thanksgiving
From: noreply@royalbank.com
Your eStatement is ready
From: support@amazon-order-help.co
Action required on your order
From: deals@shoppers-flyers.com
This week's Shoppers Drug Mart flyer
From: cra-tax-refund@cra.gc.ca-refund.info
Your CRA refund is pending
From: no-reply@canadapost.ca
Your package is out for delivery
📥 Keep in Inbox
📁 Move to Spam
🗑️ Delete Immediately
✉️

Key tip: Always look at the full email address, not just the name. Scammers can make the display name say "Apple" or "CRA" — but the actual address gives them away.
